## Repair notes

**1 is the finding that would have propagated a lie.** The Batch 0005 prompt was written by the writer phase and could not know that no review had run; it asserted a repair that had not happened, in the same paragraph that warns against exactly that failure for batches 0002 and 0003. The sentence now points at this file instead of at a claim about the past. `reviews/README.md` says review files are created after the first batch, and `batch-0001` has one; batch 0003's repair pass did not leave one, which is why the absence of a file was read as evidence.

**2, clocks.** Chapter 35 now runs: the yard dark at the fourth hour, Teale's delivery at the low gate at the seventh, the watch-book entry at the eighth, Oren and Tomas in the yard at the ninth (which is what Tomas's own day-book entry always said), and the watch house at the tenth. The dark-at-four paragraph was moved to the top of the same section so that it reads as an earlier beat of the evening rather than the frame of the walk. Chapter 32 now runs: the copper at the fourth, Crake at the fifth, the map taken at ten past the eleventh, the watch house at the twelfth, and the Bellhouse yard at about the ninth hour of the night, which is the hour the closing day-book entry was already stamped with.

**3 and 4, documents.** In Ch 34 Oren now reads the **first five** paragraphs of the covering page to Ardle and Cask before he signs it and the sixth afterwards, and says in front of ninety people that he is going to read the sixth too; the court scene already read the page "in full, twice, paragraph by paragraph", and it now matches. Ardle no longer reads "the sixth paragraph" of a page that has not been written yet; he reads the sixth **name**. The list is two pages, the returned packet is the covering page plus those two pages, and the return is brought back the same evening by the district's own rider, which is what the district's margin note and Oren's closing entry both already required.

**5, the reversal.** Two years, not eleven days. Mrs Wilde now says her mother gave her answer at that door two autumns ago and that there has been a round at that door every month since and none of them has cleared it out; Collis's return says the name "has been in the same field for two years, through every round since"; and Tomas's day-book entry at house 108 gives two years for the mother's death and for the household's two-year attempt to get the name struck. The Crown's procedure is unchanged and the horror is larger: the office is not mistaking an old name for a new one, it is reading a name it has carried forward for two years as a witness to this round.

**6, the keepers.** Renn and Cray no longer accept at the board before the bell exists. At the board they hear the words and say they will answer a bell that has been cast; Cray names two different doors and makes him read them back twice. The written acceptances now happen once, in the asking sequence, second and third, in the words that were already on the page. Tamsin's foreknowledge was rewritten from possession to reading of people — she tells him who is coming who will say yes and why — because she is asked first and cannot have been told. The cost entered at the board is that the barrel holds metal for one bell and not for two; the barrel is empty after the pour.

**8, the mouth of the fish.** The form asks for both. The chapter now says so and gives the reason the second half matters: an office can demand a keeper list again on a form with a box for it, and cannot get a list of everyone who has spoken in public in one stroke, so it asked for both on one sheet so the second would arrive filed under the first. Paragraph Four of the covering page — *produces no keeper list, and would produce one on a return framed as keepers* — is now consistent with the form instead of contradicting it.

Chapter 37 has three bench strikes with a leather an hour apart, one strike in the open, one ring at the fifth hour of the afternoon and one at the seventh; Chapter 38 has Renn once at the low drain gate, Cray once at the ward board, and four strikes in the market lane. **Twelve entries on the nineteenth, not eight**, and the state files now say so with the breakdown. The drain bar was given at the ninth hour (Ch 37), not the twelfth. The seventh hour of the nineteenth is the evening, because the water came at the sixth hour of the evening, so no state file attaches "of the afternoon" to it. Ardle and Cask man the north gate on the eighteenth to the twenty-first (Ch 35); Teale says in public that he will be at that gate at the sixth hour of the twenty-second and at the seventh and eighth (Ch 40). **14 is a change, not a slip, and it is now on the page.** Chapter 29 established that nobody in Kells was to be told, because a courier who says her note is gone is a courier whose bell is free. Chapter 40 has her say the fourth of her four reasons in a lane, and about nine people hear that it is the real one; the repair keeps that and makes the rest explicit. She then says the rest of it out loud, on her own initiative, and tells about a hundred people to write it down, on the reasoning that **a thing said once by the person it belongs to is a record and a thing found out about in a lane is a rumour**, and that the next person who wants her bell will otherwise have heard the rumour and not her. She prices it herself and refuses to be asked whether she thought about it. `state/continuity.md` carries it as a **deliberate canon change** with what it does and does not alter: she has rung nothing, the bell and the route are still hers, and nothing has been rescued for her; but her deafness is now on the ward's record and can be priced by anyone who wants her bell. The Day 13 rule in the continuity file is marked as superseded rather than deleted.

**14 was a judgement call and it is the one finding here that could have gone the other way.** Cutting the disclosure would have preserved the Day 13 rule and cost the volume its best version of Tamsin in the batch's closing chapter. It is recorded as deliberate so that the next writer may use it rather than re-derive it.
