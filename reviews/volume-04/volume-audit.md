**Method.** The audit worked from the summaries and the state layer first and opened prose second, so that a claim in a state file was never treated as evidence about a chapter. Every number, date, clock time, custody state, physical condition, age and name below is cited to a chapter, and **where a chapter is ambiguous, the chapter's version is recorded and the ambiguity is declared rather than resolved.** The six lessons of `state/continuity.md` §10r were carried as required method and not as encouragement, and **the cheapest instrument in this repository is a person reading a finished file**, which is why all ten closing chapters were read in full and why four of the eleven findings below were found by reading and not by a script.

**Two instruments are named here because a figure without a method is not a measurement.** The **duplicate instrument** is: markdown emphasis and blockquote marks stripped, case-folded, words tokenised as `[A-Za-z0-9'’-]+`, and for two paragraphs the *longest common contiguous word run*, compared over every **distinct** paragraph pair inside a file (test one) and over all 1,225 file pairs (test three). **A paragraph is never compared with itself**, which is a defect the first version of this audit's own instrument had and which is named here because it produced a false batch maximum of eighty and had to be rebuilt. The **near-duplicate instrument** is: `difflib` word alignment between two units, blocks merged across gaps of eight words or fewer, a region of twenty words or more flagged when at least 70% of its words align and the longest *exact* run inside it is under twenty. This is the §10r lesson-3 test and it is what the exact test cannot see.
