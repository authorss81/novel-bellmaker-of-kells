**Method.** The audit worked from the summaries and the state layer first and opened prose second, so that a claim in a state file was never treated as evidence about a chapter. Every number, date, clock time, custody state, physical condition, age and name below is cited to a chapter. **Where a chapter is ambiguous, the chapter's version is recorded and the ambiguity is declared rather than resolved.**

**Scope discipline.** This phase did not rewrite the volume. **Seventeen chapters were touched, in 38 insertions and 110 deleted lines, and every deleted line is one of four things and nothing else:** twenty-nine `##` section headings, twenty-nine `---` rules that served them, the blank lines around them, and **thirteen full-line replacements** — nine of them day-book re-derivations rewritten so that the day-book re-derives instead of re-printing, one of them a figure, one a trade duration, and two the numerals in a job-count. **No sentence of prose was cut, no event, motive, refusal, document, custody state, scene, chapter title or day moved, and no batch was restarted.**

## Repair notes

**1. The headings, and why the earlier record was right that they were there.** `state/continuity.md` §10g measured Batch 0004 at seventy-three headings in ten chapters and removed them, and it recorded at the end that the same scaffolding had been used in Batch 0003 at zero to four a chapter and that §10e reviewed that batch and did not raise it. **Both halves of that were true and the second half is the one that matters: a review pass looked at Batch 0003 and did not see it, because the headings read as chapter prose and a reviewer reading for continuity does not count them.** This audit counted them mechanically across all three volumes, which is the only reason the debt was found. **The measurement is `grep -c '^## '` per chapter file, and it is now zero in all one hundred and fifty chapters except four in Volume 02.**

**2. The day-book, and the difference between a motif and a re-derivation.** The rule §10c set is that the day-book must re-derive and not transcribe, and the rule §10h set for this repair is that a motif or ritual sentence is not to be touched and a repeat a later chapter must carry forward word for word stays. **Sorting the ten runs into those two kinds is the whole judgement in this repair, and the sort is: a run that is the volume's structural furniture stays, and a run that is the narrator's finding coming back in the man's own hand the same afternoon is broken.** The furniture, by chapter: the carrier's specification and the custody formula (0141, 0143), the fifth clause's wording (0142), one of the four sentences of a bell's claim (0144), the seed sentence about a route that answers (0145), her sentence in 0146, the Office-is-right-about-the-procedure line (0147), the fourth clause of the arrangement (0148), the hundred-and-twelfth's habit (0149), the best-thing framing (0150). **The re-derivations, by chapter: two in 0143, three in 0145, one in 0146, three in 0147, four in 0149, one in 0150.** Every one of the nine was rewritten so that the day-book reaches the same finding by a different road, and in three of them the new sentence is better than the copy it replaces — **and this note was corrected on review, because it first described the 0145 repair wrongly.
