# Chapter 0099 — The King Is Not Defeated

He had been in this town twenty-eight days and he had asked six people in it questions and been answered correctly six times, and on the morning of the eighty-fifth day of the month he worked out the thing that had been sitting behind every one of those six answers since the fifty-eighth, and it took him about nine minutes and it was this:

**Nobody had asked for the finding.**

The district's finding of the twenty-eighth was a document. It said that the ward's bells were unaccounted for. It had been withdrawn on the thirty-third by a surveyor on her own paper, and the withdrawal said that his district had been wrong about a set of bells, and there were about nine hundred bells in that district, and **three hundred people could ask for that document and in seven weeks in four counties not one person had asked.**

He had it in his head the way a man has a stone in his boot. He had had it in his boot since the forty-second day of the month, when a district officer in a real office in a real town had read it out to him and said it was not a win, and he had written it down on the same day with the hour against it and had not done anything about it since, for the reason that a competent woman had given him on a landing in this county six days after that, and that he had watched himself not ask for it and had been told why he did not, and had not gone round to the other end of it in twenty days.

---

He got to the front of the clerk's hall at about the fourth hour and stood in the street, and the door was open, and it was the fourth hour of a market day and the square was full and about nine hundred people were buying cheese.

He did not go in and ask for it.

That is the whole of the eighty-fifth up to about the seventh hour and it is the first thing in six weeks he has written down as a decision instead of a finding, and the reason is at the foot of the entry and it is not a good one and he does not pretend it is:

**A finding is a document. I have made four records in six weeks about persons who did not ask me for one, and one of them cost a household the front of a row of nine pitches, and the man who cost it is right, and the man who made it is me.** A woman on a landing in this county told me on the sixty-fifth that she would put her name to a practice and that stopping a practice was worth the rest of this month and every month after it and was not worth the ninth hour of the evening of the sixty-ninth, and she told me why I did not ask her for it, and I did not ask her, and I have not gone round to the other end of it in three weeks and I am not going to start on the eighty-fifth because I have run out of things to do.

And a man who has run out of things to do asks an office for a piece of paper, and a piece of paper is the only thing he can be certain of, and I wrote that in this book on the seventy-seventh at about the ninth hour of the evening and I have not improved it since.

---

At about the seventh hour he went in, because a hall is a building and a building has a door and the door was open, and the notice on the wall nine feet inside it says that anybody may ask a clerk about the work, and it means it, and about four men in this kingdom have ever put a boot through it.

Bryn Pethick had ink on the side of his hand again and put a chair at the wrong side of the counter, which Tomas Vale had now seen him do three times and had stopped trying to understand.

“Sit down. **You have not asked me anything in twelve days and I have noticed, and I have not decided what to make of it, and I have not made anything of it, and I would like it noticed that deciding not to make something of a man is not a kindness and takes me about nine seconds.**”

“I have come about the finding of the district.”

And Bryn Pethick put the pen down, which he did not do for anything, and looked at him, and Tomas Vale understood inside about a second and a half that he had made a mistake of about nine seconds' standing and that it was the first one he had made in this building in twelve days.

“**Say the rest of it,**” said the clerk.

“**The finding of the twenty-eighth. The district's. Withdrawn on the thirty-third by a surveyor on her own paper. It is a document three hundred people can ask for and in seven weeks in four counties not one person has asked, and I have been carrying it since the forty-second and I have not asked, and I want it.**”

“No,” said Bryn Pethick.

“That is one word and I have not got an answer.”

“**You have got the only answer there is and it took me about nine seconds and you knew it before you got through the door, and I am going to give it you straight because you have asked me properly and because I have said the same true sentence to you twice and written it down twice, and I am tired of watching you carry it.**

“**That finding is not in this building. It never was. It is about a ward in another county, in a district in another county, and about nine hundred bells in a district that is not this one, and there is about nine hundred lines a year coming into this hall and not one of the nine hundred is that document. You cannot ask me for it because it is not this hall's business, and if I went and got it for you I would be doing a thing nobody has authority to do in a building where anybody may ask a clerk about the work.**”

“Then where is it.”

“**In a building in another county, in a file, on a shelf, in about a day and a half's road, and you can write for it, and there is a form for writing, and anybody may use the form, and the form will be answered inside a month, and it will be answered correctly, and about three hundred people in that county could have used that form since the thirty-third and not one of them has.**”

---

“**And here is the whole of it, and I am going to say it once and I am not going to improve it, and I wrote it down on the seventy-third and gave it to you because I could not save the ink, and I am not going to be able to unwrite it: a document nobody has asked for is a thing that has not happened.**”

“That is fair.”

“**You have had that since the fortieth. You carried it eighteen days before you ever came into this hall and you put it into four counties and four buildings, and a clerk in this building said it to you in nine seconds on the sixty-second and went back to a column and has not thought about it since, and you made it into a key, and about four hundred people in this county heard that you had been told what a document is and every one of them heard it as a key.**

“**And it has been forty-five days and nobody has asked for anything, and you are not going to, and I have watched you three times now in this street not going in, and I want to say one thing about that and then I am going to go back to my column and I am not going to enjoy saying it.**”

“Say it.”

“**It is not a virtue. A man who carries a sentence for forty-five days and never once asks anybody to put it in a place is not careful. He is a man who has found out that a document is the only thing he can be certain of, and who has not found a household yet that wants to be certain of, and who is therefore still carrying it about, and I have been in this building about four years and I have never once seen anybody do that and I have read about four hundred sheets off this counter and not one of them was written by anybody who had to be right.**”

---

“And there is a thing in this building I am not going to ask for, and I am going to tell you that I know it is in here, and I am not going to say how, and you are not going to tell me, and I want it entered that I did not ask.”

Bryn Pethick did not move.

“**Say nothing else about that. Say it and stop.**”

“No,” said Tomas Vale. “**I am going to say one half of it and I am not going to say the other half and the half I am going to say is a fact about me and not about you, and it is this: it went down the water on the fifty-fifth at about the second hour of the afternoon, in an auditor's own book, in her own name at the head of it, with the hour against it, and it has been in this building since about the fifty-eighth, and I have not gone to look for it and I did not know it was here until a man in a vat room told me on the eighty-second that a fire had been out in two rooms for a fortnight.**”

Nobody said anything for about nine seconds.

“**And the reason I am not asking for it is not the fifth clause of a receipt I signed in another county. She is not a household. I am stopping myself, and I wrote that down on the seventy-eighth and I have not improved it, and the whole of it is five words: she did not ask me.**”

“I have made four records in six weeks about persons who did not ask me for one, and one of them cost a household the front of a row of nine pitches, and the man who made that record was me and nobody has forgiven anybody and nobody has apologised to anybody.”

“**And I am not going to go into a building in this town and ask for a woman's answer to a question she was asked in a room in another county, on the fortieth, by a man who has carried it nowhere and shown it to nobody and asked for it nowhere, and I am not going to do it for her and I am not going to do it to her, and if she ever wants to know whether it arrived she will have to ask somebody who is not me.**”

---

“**One more thing and then go out of my light, because I have about nine hundred lines in front of me and none of them are in a document nobody has asked for.**”

“Go on, then.”

“**A sheet went out of this hall on the eighty-first in a satchel, on a form, with five numbers on a slip, and one of the five was a carbon of a form of the second series with a reading-out on the back in a private hand, and there was a man with a barrow in the room and the man with the barrow did not say one word.**

“**I want it entered that nothing in this building has been altered because of that man's two days here, and that is not a favour to him and I would like it understood. Nothing in this building has been altered because of a sheet of his.**”

“How do you know that.”

“**Because I have looked, and looking is not in my job.**”

“How long have you been looking.”

“**Since the seventy-ninth. And I did not do it for him and I did not tell him and I am telling him now because he has just told me about a thing in this building that he has not asked for, and it seems to me that if a man can do that in a room then I can do this in a corridor, and neither of us is going to be thanked for it and I am not going to be thanked for it and I am not going to let it become a thing he is praised for, because I have been in this building about four years and I know exactly what a thing like that turns into in about a year.**”

---

And then Tomas Vale asked him the question, in the words a woman gave him on a road in another county in the rain on the fortieth day of the month, because it was the only question he had and because a hall is not a person and a person is the only thing that can be asked and can refuse.

“**What is the document.**”

And Bryn Pethick did not take eleven seconds and did not pause.

“**It is a record of the answer given, entered in the answering column against the line, in the order in which the answers were given. It is the only thing it is. It is not a household's answer and it is not a person's answer and it is not a copy of either one, and the office obtains an answer from a person and the entry is made by the office, and the office is not in dispute between anybody in this room.**”

He wrote it standing up, on the hall's own paper, in his own hand, with the hour against it, and he signed it and gave it across, and Tomas Vale did not take it.

“Do not give me that.”

“**I have already written it.**”

“You wrote it on the seventy-third and you gave it to me and I am not going to tell you where I have put it, and it is the fourth time you have said that sentence to me in about nine seconds and it is the fifth time it has been given in four counties and four of the five times it was said by a person who did not have to say it, and I am not going to take a fifth one off you and put it in a coat with four, and I am not going to ask you for a second copy and I am not going to let you offer me one, and here is why, and I want it noticed that I have thought about it in about nine seconds and I have got it wrong twice today already.**

“**Two versions of the same position in the same hand can differ. You said that to me about a copy of a sheet and you were right and I have been thinking about it for a fortnight. And the seventy-third is one sheet, in one hand, in this county, and I am not going to write it a second time, and I am not going to let anybody talk me into writing it a second time, and if you want to know what is in it then you can read it, and if you want me to write it again the answer is no, and that is a clerk's whole trade and it is not mine.**”

And Bryn Pethick put his hand flat on the counter and said:

“**That is the whole of my trade and I have never said it to anybody.**”

---

He wrote the last entry of the eighty-fifth at about the ninth hour of the evening, on the step of the draper's shop, in the flat hand, with the hours, and he filled the first and third columns and left the second and the fourth as they were.

> *The eighty-fifth. **The Crown has not been defeated and I was never going to defeat it, and I want it written in the plainest words I have and I am not going to improve either of them.** I have been in this town twenty-eight days with a barrow and a frame with four bolts in it and thirteen documents and a woman who does not write things down, and here is the whole of what has happened and it is all of it:*
>
> *A finding was withdrawn on the thirty-third by a surveyor on her own paper, and about three hundred people in another county could ask for it, and in seven weeks and four counties not one person has asked, **and the reason is that it is in a building two days' road away and there is a form for asking and the form is correct and the form will be answered in a month, and a document nobody has asked for is a thing that has not happened, and I have had that sentence for forty-five days and I have never once used it, and a clerk in that building gave it to me in nine seconds on the sixty-second and went back to a column.***
>
> *An officer was relieved of nothing, because she was never given anything to hold. She can put her name to a practice and she has, and a practice in one district is stopped in writing, and it is worth the rest of this month and every month after it, and it is not worth the ninth hour of the evening of the sixty-ninth and nobody is going to pretend it is.*
>
> *A room over a draper's shop was taken in about three hours, lawfully, by a man with a form, and there was no room in the form, and the reason there was no room in the form is the fifth clause of a receipt I asked a clerk to add to in another county, and the same clause is the reason five sheets went out of that room, and neither the officer nor the man in charge of the shelves knows that, and I know it, and it is the only thing in six weeks that is mine and it is not an advantage.*
>
> *An office answered a question it did not have to answer, out loud, in a room, in a stranger's own name, and I made a page about it after he asked me in a doorway not to, and he has a house on a street in this town and a wife and a boy and he is right and he is not sorry and he is not brave and he is not going to say it again.*
>
> *And a set of rolls is on a shelf in a real building in this town. About eleven thousand lines a year go into halls like that one across that district and about nine hundred a year go into that one, and the answers are ours and the names go up, and a clerk of four years has never been upstairs and is not going to be, and asked me on the seventy-third to have it written down that I asked him where the names go and that he said no, twice, and the second no meant *nobody has ever asked me, and now you have, and I am going to do nothing*, and he is right.*
>
> ***And a clerk of eleven years has not altered one thing in that building because of a sheet of mine, and he did not do it for me and he did not tell me and he told me in a corridor, and looking is not in his job. **That is the one thing in six weeks that nobody can take back and nobody has paid for, and I did not ask for it and I am not going to be thanked for it and I have written it down so that if it is ever said to me that nothing came of six weeks in this county, there is one line here that says otherwise, and I am not going to be the man who uses it either.***
>
> *Third column, ruled apart, the hours flat against them, and I am merging nothing: **a hall is not a person and never was, and a habit of carrying is the only thing I have ever been able to do about a hall, and it took me forty-five days, four counties, four buildings, a receipt, a bell, a market, a room and a form to find out that I have been talking to a building.***
>
> *And the third question. **How a Crown office turns one clerk's answer into a whole settlement's consent. It does not need to. A roll does not need anybody's answer; a duty is a mouth; a mouth is for a claim; and the claim is made in a queue, in about nine minutes, by about eleven thousand people a year who are not clerks.** And that is answered and it is the whole of forty-five days.*
>
> *And the other one is not answered. **Where names go when they are registered. Nobody has told me, and a district officer in another county asked his district twice in writing and had no answer to either, and a man in a vat room took nine lines out of a room on the eighty-second and the nine lines were numbers, and a number is a filing reference and not a man, and that is a smaller thing than I have been carrying and it is the only thing I have learned, and I am not going to pretend it is an answer.***
>
> ***All five clauses stand. Nothing was rung. The frame is four streets off with four bolts made fast in it and no clapper, and it has not been in a room. The bell in the sack was rung once in this town and is not certified for anything and I have not touched it in eight days and I do not know where it went. The bar on my own bell is a bar in another county and my own bell is in a sealed yard in Kells and it stays there. My thirteen documents are thirteen. I did not take a fifth copy of a clerk's sentence and I am not going to and I have written down why, twice, and one of those is a sentence he said and one of them is mine and they are not the same and I have not merged them.***
>
> *And it is raining. **It has been raining since about the seventh hour and it is going to keep raining and there is a road out of this town tomorrow and a barrow on it that I cannot pay with and a frame in it that cannot be rung and a bell in a sack that I have rung once, and there is a woman beside me who has not asked me for anything since the seventy-second and is not going to, and I have no standing anywhere in this county and no reason to be in it and four things that I got here that I did not have when I came, and no room at all since the eighty-second, and a yard behind a draper's shop with the rain going into it.***
