# Chapter 0125 — The Duty And Not The Sentence

It was the fourth hour of the hundred and eleventh day and Tomas Vale went down the north corridor of a building on the middle terrace and a clerk at a desk with a lamp looked up at him and did not look down again, because a man who comes into a room two days running is a different thing from a man who comes into a room once.

“**You again.**”

“**I am.**”

“**I am not going to ask you why, and there is no box on this building that would let me, and I have been in this room about four years and I have never wanted to know why anybody stands at that rail.**”

He put his pen down.

“**Anybody may read it. That has never once been in question and it is not in question now. **And I am going to say the other half of that, because you have come twice and a man who comes twice gets the whole of it, and if I only gave you the half that lets you in I would be doing you a disservice and I have got about four hours and a lamp and nothing else to do.**”**

“**Say it.**”

“**A record is the court's and it is not a copy of anything. **If you want a copy, you have to ask the court for a copy, and the court does not give copies of a record of a matter to a person who is not a party to it.**”

He said the last part without any weight on it at all.

“**That is not me. That is the practice and it goes back further than this room. And I want to say the next sentence and I want to say it in the right order. **I am not being careful with you. I would have told a man in a market in a fortnight if there had been anything in it worth carrying to a market.** A record is the court's. It is on that rail. It is not yours, and it is not hers, and the only person in this kingdom who can turn a record into a piece of paper that travels is a court, and a court does it for a party.**”

---

“**Then I am not going to ask for one.**”

“**No. And I am not going to pretend you wanted to, because everybody wants to, and wanting it is not a thing I am going to write down for you.**”

**And Tomas Vale stood at the rail with nothing in his hands, which is a sentence that has been true for a hundred and eleven days, and undid the string on a bundle of about forty sheets, and read the four hundred words again because he is a man who compares, and then went to the back, and opened the fold.**
**And the sheet inside the fold is a different paper from the rest, and it is eleven lines long, and it is in her words and not in her hand.**

> *I will answer once. I told the court I would and I am doing it and I am not going to be asked again in this room.*
>
> *The first thing is that a person is not the only thing that carries an answer, and I did not know that until I read four sills over four nights, and I wrote down what I read, and I did not take the sheet to anybody for nine days.*
>
> *What I read was this. A man's answer going out from a place where he was not standing, in his own voice, and him not knowing that it had gone. Not a bell heard twice by a wall. **The same words, in the same order, in a hand the shape of his, in a district two days' road off, and nobody there able to say it was not his, because it was his.***
>
> *That is the whole of what I found. I found it by reading. Nobody told me and I have not got a name to give anybody and I am not going to be asked for one.*
>
> *The second thing is mine and I am not going to be sorry for it in this room or out of it.*
>
> *When I had the sheet I did not take it to the district.*
>
> *I took nine of the things and I hid them and I hid them myself.*
>
> ***I hid the first Crown clappers.***
>
> *I have said it out loud in a room with a clerk in it and a man writing. It is in his book and it was not in mine, and I watched him write it, and I want that entered in the order it happened, and it happened before he wrote it.*
>
> *Nine of them. I put them where a man making a bell would not go. It took eleven days and I have counted the eleven a great many times since and I would do it again and I would do it sooner.*
>
> *After that I answered nothing else to anybody about the nine, or the sheet, or where they went, and I am not starting today, because you have asked me four times on four days and I said yes once, and once is what I said I would do.*

---

**That is the whole of it. Eleven lines and two things and then it stops.**

It stops in the middle of a subject. It stops the way a record stops, which is to say that it does not stop at all; **it is simply that there is nothing after it, and the nothing is not marked, and there is no rule anywhere that says a record has to say what comes next.**

And the record of the matter, in four hundred words in the office's own hand above it, says that nothing has been entered that was not said in this room, and that the court has not found that she did or did not do the thing charged, and that the matter is closed.

**And this is the finding of the hundred and eleventh day, and it is four inches long, and he stood up and worked it out in about nine minutes with his hands on the rail.**

**A duty is a thing a person accepts. A conviction is a thing a person suffers. They are not the same shape and they are not the same size and one of them fits in a person's mouth and the other one is done to a person in a room they are not in.**

**And the two are not the same difference, and that is the whole of what the Office did, and here it is, in four inches:**

*They kept the duty and they cut the rest.*

That is all. There is no conspiracy in it and no villain in it and no man in it. **A clerk took a woman's answer and entered the part of it that answered the charge, and the part of it that answered the charge was that she took the nine and hid them, and that is a duty, and it is nine words in her own words, and it is true, and it is the only thing in the whole of the record that is anybody's at all.**

**And a court cannot enter a sentence on a form that has no box for a sentence, and a form that has four boxes does not have a box for a sentence, and so the sentence — if there was one — went in a box, and the box is empty, and on the other nine forms in that tray the box is not empty.**

**And the Office did not cut the duty. The Office kept the duty, on purpose, because the duty was the answer to the charge, and a record that says a person refused to answer is worth nothing, and a record that says a person answered is worth a great deal, and they wanted the worth of it. And they did not know — they cannot have known, there is nobody above a district who would tell them — that keeping it was the evidence.**

**A document that cut the whole of it would have been a document about an absence, and a document about an absence can be argued with in a market, and a document that keeps a duty and cuts an outcome is a document about a matter that went well for the district.**

---

**And there is a second thing in that fold and it is worse, and he had known it since he was a young man in a yard and it has taken a hundred and eleven days and a piece of paper to make it mean anything.**

The eleven lines are in her words.

**The hand is not hers.**

He does not know whose hand it is and he has not got a way of finding out and there is no form in this kingdom for finding out whose hand is in a file, and there is not one in a store either.

> *A thing that comes out twice is a claim and a copy of a claim. **A copy is how a sentence stops belonging to the person who said it.** I have known that since I was in a yard with a bell inside eight feet of hard stone, and I have written it down twice, and I have never once had it apply to a person I was related to.*
>
> **These are her words. They are in a fair hand, in a file, on a rail, in a room anybody may walk into, and the reason they are hers is that the clerk says so at the top of the record, and a clerk is a man who was in the room.**
>
> *And the copy is the reason the thing has stopped being hers. Not because anybody lied. **Because a copy of an answer is an answer that has left the person who gave it, and once it has left, it is nobody's, and it is a thing in a file about a matter in a district, and it says what she did and it does not say who she was, and that is not a thing anybody did to her. It is what a copy is.**

**And he did not ask.** Not of Hale, not of the man who swept the corridor, not of the woman with a tray, and not of himself, out loud, in a public room, in a city of about nine thousand people.

And he wrote down that he did not, at about the seventh hour, on the low wall at the end of the four steps, and the reason is four inches long and it is not cowardice and it is not care:

> *A question asked in a public room is a question that is in the room. **About forty people a week come through that door and every one of them is a person somebody with a column can ask, this month or in two years, about a thing a stranger said at a rail.** I have done that to other people in four counties. I have put a question in a lane in a market town and had a man answer it out loud to nine people and it has followed him since.*
>
> *And there is nobody on that wall to protect. **She is not on it. She is not on that board and she is not in that room and no clerk in it has ever said her name out loud, and if I put the question in there then I have put her in there, in the only way available to me, which is by making a stranger care about her for about nine seconds.** And that is a thing I am not entitled to do with a woman who has not asked me one thing in six years.

**And the absence, which is the finding, and which is not a question and not a cliff, and which he wrote out flat because it is the only thing in a hundred and eleven days he cannot put down:**

> ***The record does not say one word about whether she was imprisoned, or killed, or used as an anchor.***
>
> *It does not say it in her words, because she was not asked and she would not have answered. It does not say it in a clerk's mouth, because no clerk has said it to me and I have not asked one. It is not in the four boxes, because the fourth box is empty. It is not in a margin, because there are no margins. It is not in a form, because there is no form in this kingdom for what became of a person who has been tried.*
>
> ***And I am not going to write the three of them out one under the other tonight, and I want that on the page, because a man who writes three things down in a book has made a list, and a list is a thing that can be taken out of the book and put in front of him in four years by somebody with a column. I have got one of these already and I did not know it was a list.***
>
> **It is not there. That is the whole of what I know. He came for a woman and he has got a duty, and a duty is a thing she did, and a sentence is a thing that was done to her, and I am holding the first one and I will never be given the second one, and the difference between the two of them is a line somebody crossed out of a form in an office, and I am not going to be told by a stranger which of the two she is.**

---

**And at about the ninth hour of the evening he was on a step at the foot of the top stair with the tarpaulin over the cradle and the four bolts made fast, and he undid the string on that bundle at the seventh hour and tied it back and it is on a rail in a room I was in today and it will be there when I am gone and nobody has taken anything and nobody has been asked for anything.**

He wrote the last entry of the hundred and eleventh, and he filled the first, second and third columns and left the fourth as it was.

> *The hundred and eleventh. **A duty and not a sentence, and the difference between them is a line somebody did not take.**
>
> *I got the whole of it today, all four hundred words and the fold at the back, and it is eleven lines in her words and it says two things. It says she found, by reading four sills over four nights, that answers were being copied — a man's answer going out of a place he was not standing in, in his own voice, and him not knowing. **And it says she took the nine and hid them herself, and she said it out loud in front of a clerk, and the clerk wrote it down.***
>
> *Second column, and the whole of what I came up four flights for. **They kept the duty and they cut the rest.** Not a sentence. Not a cell. Not a day. Nothing. The box where a sentence would be is the fourth box and it is empty, and on the other nine forms in a tray nine feet off it the fourth box is full, **and nobody crossed it out, and a form that has been argued with has a line through it, and this form has not been argued with. Somebody chose it and then went home at the sixth hour.**
>
> *And here is the part I did not have until this morning. **A duty is a thing a person accepts. A conviction is a thing a person suffers.** They are not the same shape. One of them is nine words in her own mouth and the other one is a thing done to a person in a room she was not in, and the Office has kept the first and thrown away the second, **and it did not know that keeping it was the evidence, and it could not have known, because there is nobody above a district who tells a clerk what an empty fourth box is worth.**
>
> *And the district's own list, four streets and four flights up, has a word for the thing in the fourth column of its ninth hundred line, and the word is **coupling.** A word about a piece of metal. **Somebody who cut four hundred words out of a record knew the word for it, and the store's book still says coupling, and there are nine hundred lines in that book and not one of them says what any of the ninety words is for.**
>
> *Third column, ruled apart, and the worse of the two. **The eleven lines are hers and the hand is not hers, and the record says at the top that nothing has been entered that was not said in this room, and I have believed the record because it is a record and I have got no way not to.** A thing that comes out twice is a copy and a copy is how a sentence stops belonging to the person who said it, **and I have known that since I was in a yard and I have never once had it apply to somebody I could name. She is on a rail in a public room and she is not on it.** That is not a thing somebody did to her. **That is what a copy is.**
>
> *And what I did not ask. **I did not ask one person in that building what became of her, and I did not ask it of myself out loud in a room, and the reason is four inches long and it is this: a question asked in a public room is a question that is in the room, and about forty people a week come through that door, and she is not on the wall and she is not in that building and no clerk in it has ever said her name out loud, and if I put the question in there then I have put her in there in the only way available to me.** I have done that to a man in a lane in a market town in four counties and it has followed him since and I was right to do it and it cost him nine days of a lighter and a cart. **I am not entitled to do it to her, and she has not asked me for one thing in six years, and she is not going to ask me for this one either.**
>
> *And the absence, which is the finding, and which I am writing a second time in my own hand because a thing said once in a book is a thing I might have got wrong. **It is not there. It is not in her words and it is not in a clerk's mouth and it is not in the four boxes and it is not in a margin and it is not in a form, because there is no form in this kingdom for what became of a person who has been tried. I am not going to write the three of them out one under the other, because a man who writes three things down in a book has made a list.**
>
> *All five clauses stand. **Nothing rung, and nothing copied, and nothing asked of anybody, and the bundle is tied the way I found it.** Four bolts, and the frame has not been in a building since the thirty-third and I stood in a room for most of today and it makes no difference to the frame. The carrier in the sack untouched, thirty-four days, and I do not know where either of my two public ringings went and I am not going to find out. My own bell is in a sealed yard in Kells and I am not going to it. **Thirteen documents in a coat, and a court record that is the court's and is on a rail in a public room, and I read the whole of it standing up and took nothing out of it, and those are two correct figures and neither of them is a fourteenth.**
>
> *Which office is it. **It is a clerk who wrote down what he was given and a form with four boxes and one of them left empty on purpose, and a woman who hid nine things for eleven days and said so in a room and was not asked anything else.** And I have got the most important document of my life and it is in my head and there is nothing whatever in my hands, **and I am the only person in this kingdom who knows exactly how it can be got wrong, and nobody will take it off me, and that is not a comfort and it is not a punishment. It is the trade.**
