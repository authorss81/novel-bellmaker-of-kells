# Chapter 0134 — Three Sets Of Terms And No Reunion

It was the fourth hour of the hundred and twentieth day and Tomas Vale was on a step by the river on the flat with the barrow above him on the bank and the tarpaulin over the cradle, and the rope was hanging off the frame of the net-loft where it hangs, and nobody had it in their hands.

**He had not asked her to take her hands off it and she had not offered. It is nine feet long and it is worn in three places and it belongs to the loft and it is the fourth thing in this business that a person can be about without saying a word about it.**

He had been in the city twenty-five days and he had a thing in his head he could not write down and could not give to anybody, and on the hundred and nineteenth a woman in a city of about nine thousand people had done a thing about it in a room he was not in, and neither of those two facts can be put next to the other one without making a story.

The woman who let the room took the money and did not ask what for and did not ask who he was and had a fire in it and a bench and a sill and a table with a plate on it that had been on the table when the room was built.

**He paid for an hour of a room above a shop in a city of about nine thousand people. There is no form in this kingdom for that either and there is not supposed to be, and the reason he paid and did not ask is four inches long: a man who is given a room is a man who owes, and a man who owes is a man somebody is kind to, and I have spent a hundred and twenty days finding out that I have not got a person in this city who is kind to me and I did not want the first one to be a woman who takes money for a room.**

He put the barrow's rail against the wall and the four bolts stayed made fast where they were made fast at the fourth hour and the frame has not been in a building since the thirty-third and it was not in this one.

> *A public signal in a place needs three independent answers, each of them free to say **yes, no, later, or this much and not that**, and each of them in its own words, and the rule that does the work is four inches long: **you set the claim down once and you read the same words to each of them in the same order, and if you improve the words for the second one because the first one frightened you, you are not collecting answers, you are arranging agreement.**
>
> *And a man can be one of the three answers. A man cannot be two of them. **The second one he is, the first one stops being an answer and becomes a man saying the same thing twice.** And there is no form in this kingdom on which a man may take the same words to three people and call the result a count, and if there were one then all three sentences would be his and not one of them would be anybody's answer.
>
> *I am a man with a barrow, no standing in any district in this city, a bar on my own bell, a frame in a cradle that cannot be rung, a hearing I could not answer, and eleven lines in a head that cannot be got off me and cannot be given to anybody. **I am one of these three people and I am not a keeper and I am not an answer twice and I am not the clerk of it, and I am not going to be the one who is owed anything out of it, and if I am, then the whole of it is a rescue and it is not.**

He set out the first set of terms in the ordinary voice, at ten feet, in a room he had paid for, and it took him about nine minutes.

“**I will not write this down.**”

“**I am not going to write it down either and I have said so twice.**”

“**I know.**”

“**Then say why, because there are three of us and two of you and a man who is paying, and a man who is paying for a room in which a thing is not written down is a man who is running it, and I have not decided to be that and if I am doing it then say so now.**”

“**Then I am running it. I am telling you that I am running it, and you can both stand up and go, and either of you can do that, and I will not follow.**”

She did not get up.

“**Go on.**”

“**The clauses are four. **One: there is no paper. Not one sheet, not one line, and not a mark on a slate, and the price side of that slate stays the other way up.** Two: I do not keep the terms. I do not know them in a form and I will not be able to produce them in four years to anybody, and if one of you says a thing tomorrow that one of you does not want said, I cannot show a paper saying she did not say it. Three: I will not take the same words to two people and come back with two of them the same.**”

“**And the fourth.**”

“**The fourth is the hard one and it is mine. **I will not ask either of you for anything.** Not today, not in a month, and not in the shape of a thing I have been wanting since the ninety-first. If either of you does a thing I will not ask what it was for and I will not write it down and I will not say thank you, and if you want to be thanked you are in the wrong room.**”

“**That is not a term. That is a man being frightened.**”

“**It is a term and I have had it since the hundred and thirteenth and I will not have it improved.**”

“**I am not going to be the second of the three.**”

“**I know.**”

“**You know and you have not said it in fourteen days and I want it on the table because a thing that is on a table can be looked at. **I am not going to be the second of anything. I am not going to be the first, and I am not going to be a count, and I am not going to be a room that three people meet in.**”

“**You said that on the hundred and sixth.**”

“**I said it on the hundred and sixth and I wrote it down and underlined it and I have not changed one word of it and I am not going to.**”

“**What is the rest of it.**”

“**I will hear a thing once. I will not hear it twice, and if you come back to me with a thing you have said to somebody else in my hearing I will know, because that is the only instrument I have ever had and I built it myself and I do not need to hear a thing twice to know it has been said twice.**”

“**That is fair and it is not a clause, it is a capability.**”

“**It is a capability and it is a clause and you know which of the two you would rather have.**”

“**Then the fourth thing. If there were three of us and we needed three answers to anything.**”

“**There are not three answers. There are two people in a room and a sheet on a wall.**”

“**There are three of us.**”

“**There are three of us and one of us is not here and has not been asked whether she would come, and I have not asked and you have not asked and the fact that neither of us has asked is the fourth thing I wanted on the table, and now it is on it.**”

**She will not be found. She will not answer. She will not be sent for, and a person who wants a thing from her may go and ask a board. Not me.**

He read it twice on the hundred and fourteenth and he has not read it a third time and he is not going to.

“**That is her terms and she set them before any of this existed and she does not know this exists.**”

“**Then it is not her terms, it is a thing she wrote about a brother with a barrow, and you are putting a woman you have not spoken to in a room by reading a sheet on a wall.**”

“**I have just said that sentence back to myself about the four inches and I have not been able to get round it and I am not going to get round it now.**”

She was quiet for a moment.

“**Say that again.**”

“**A sheet on a public wall is a thing a person said in the open for anybody. It is not a set of terms with me in it. And if I stand under a board and decide that four lines of it are her side of an arrangement, then she has been in this room since the fourteenth and she has not been asked and she has not come.**”

“**And the arrangement.**”

“**Is made on terms that survive her sentence. **Which means it is made without her, and it will hold whether she ever reads it or not, and I am not going to fetch her, and I am not going to stand under a building on her account, and if she comes in a year and wants to be in it then she will be the fourth person to a thing that was already standing and there is no form in this kingdom for that either and I will say so to her myself in the ordinary voice.**”

“**What is it, then.**”

“**A working arrangement between three people who will each say their own terms out loud once, in the open, in their own words, and there is no plate and no clerk and no form and no copy, and if the three of us are all there in a year we will do it again from the beginning and nobody will have a copy of this one.**”

“**For what.**”

“**For a thing I have got and cannot give away. **Eleven lines in a head. They cannot be got off me by anybody who wants them and they cannot be given to anybody and they cannot be made into metal and they die with me.**”

“**So we are three people protecting a sentence none of us can read.**”

“**Yes.**”

“**That is the worst arrangement I have ever been offered and I am not refusing it.**”

“**I know. I have not refused it either and I have not thanked you for it and I am not going to.**”

At about the seventh hour he worked out what the three of them are, and it took him the whole of an hour to get it out in the ordinary way, and it is not a bell and it is not a warning and it does not name what it does not know because there is no claim in it to name anything.

**A woman once said a man's own sentence back to him in a market, four inches, at ten feet, in the ordinary voice, in front of about nine hundred people, and it moved about four in ten, and it is the only instrument in this kingdom that has ever moved a line, and it is hers, and neither of the two people in this room knows it happened, and the two of them are the only two people in this kingdom who could make it work, and he is not going to say one word about it and it is not in the terms and if it ever is in the terms the arrangement is over.**

**She said in her own book on the hundred and second that the third time she writes the not-asking she would be counting, and a woman who counts is a woman waiting to be owed, and I am not owed and neither is she. A man who keeps a count of what he does not ask is a man waiting to be owed too, and I have not got a person in this city who is owed anything by me and I am not going to start a book about it.**

> *The hundred and twentieth, at about the ninth hour of the evening. **Three sets of terms and two of us in a room and the third on a wall, and no paper, and no plate, and no clerk, and I am not the one who is owed anything and I am not going to be.**
>
> *Second column, and the arrangement. **It is not a bell, it is not a signal, and it is not three answers, and the sentence about three answers is a rule about counting and not a power, and I read it back before I opened my mouth.** You set the claim down once and you read the same words to each in the same order, and if you improve the words for the second one because the first one frightened you you are arranging agreement and not collecting answers. **A man can be one of the three answers and cannot be two, and the second one he is, the first one stops being an answer. I am one of these three people and I am not a keeper and I am not an answer twice and I have a bar on my own bell.**
>
> *And my four clauses, and the fourth is the hard one and it is mine. **No paper, not one sheet, not one line, and the price side of the slate stays the other way up. I do not keep the terms and I cannot produce them in four years to anybody, and if one of them says a thing tomorrow the other one does not want said, I cannot show a paper saying she did not. I will not take the same words to two people and come back with two of them the same. And I will not ask either of them for anything, not today, not in a month, and not in the shape of a thing I have been wanting since the ninety-first, and if either of them does a thing I will not ask what it was for and I will not write it down and I will not say thank you.** She said that last one is a man being frightened. **It is a term and I have had it since the hundred and thirteenth and I will not have it improved.**
>
> *Third column, ruled apart, and hers. **I am not going to be the second of anything, and not the first, and not a count, and not a room that three people meet in. She said it on the hundred and sixth, wrote it down, underlined it, and has not changed one word in fourteen days.** And: I will hear a thing once and I will not hear it twice, and if I come back with a thing I have said to somebody else in her hearing she will know, because that is the only instrument she has ever had and she built it and she does not need to hear a thing twice to know it has been said twice.
>
> *And the third set, and it is not in this room and nobody has been asked about it. **Four drawing-pins on a public board on the top terrace, in her own hand, in her own name, addressed to nobody, and I read it twice on the hundred and fourteenth and I am not going to read it a third time.** She wrote it before any of this existed and she does not know this exists, **and a sheet on a public wall is a thing a person said in the open for anybody at all, and the whole of my error this afternoon was to decide that four lines of it were her half of a bargain with me, and if I do that in a room then I have put a woman I have not spoken to inside a room by reading a wall, and that is a thing I will not do.** So the arrangement stands or falls on her sheet without her in it, **and it does not need her, and I am not going to fetch her, and if she ever wants in she will be the fourth person to a thing that was already standing and I will say that to her in the ordinary voice myself.**
>
> *And the arrangement is three people protecting a sentence none of us can read, and the reason there is no plate is that a plate is cut for about nine hundred forms a year in a printer's room two streets off and nobody has cut one for this and nobody is going to, **and that is the reason it is safe and it is not the reason it is good.**
>
> *The count is eight and it stays eight and I did not put a question on the slate and I have written it down once in this book about a week ago and I am not going to write it down again, **because she said in her own book on the hundred and second that the third time she writes the not-asking she will be counting, and a man who keeps a count of what he does not ask is a man waiting to be owed, and I am not owed and neither is she.**
>
> *All five clauses stand. **Nothing rung, and nothing bound, and no stage, and nothing counted into iron, and no claim made in a bell and no answers collected.** Four bolts, and the frame has not been in a building since the thirty-third and it was not in a room over a shop this afternoon because a room over a shop is a building and I have paid for an hour of it and it is still a building, and I want that written down because I have been in a room today and the frame has not been in one. The carrier in the sack untouched, forty-three days, and I do not know where either of my two public ringings went and I am not going to find out. My own bell is in a sealed yard in Kells and I am not going to it. **Her bell is in her bag.**
>
> *The register is thirteen. **Thirteen documents in a coat and none of them is mine and there is no paper in this room and there is not going to be one, and the only one of the three sets of terms that is a document is on a public wall in this city and it is not mine and it is not coming down four flights.** None of them is a fourteenth and I have created nothing today, which is the first time in five weeks that a day has ended and I have got a document and I have got one fewer than I had this morning because I wrote one sentence out loud instead of in a book.
>
> *Which office is it. **It is a room above a shop with a fire in it and a plate on the table that has been on that table since the room was built, and two people in it, and a sheet on a wall four streets and four flights up with a third person's terms on it in her own hand, and no form anywhere in this kingdom for a set of terms between three people, and no plate cut for one, and no clerk, and no copy, and a man with a barrow who wanted to be the clerk of it and is not going to be.** The Office has a copy of one of the three of us' own true sentence with his name at the head of it, on a board in a market, and about two hundred people read it in about nine minutes, and a man in a room off a lane is in there over it. **And the three of us are not an answer to a matter and not a count and not a network, and I have got one of the three answers and not two and not three, and there is no form in this kingdom on which I could be two.***
