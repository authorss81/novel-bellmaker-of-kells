# Chapter 0106 — The Box For The Person Who Repeated It

She did not ask him about the leaf and he did not tell her about the leaf, and that is the first four hours of the ninety-second day of the month, and it is on the page because for once in a market she wrote nothing down about him.

She had a slate under her arm with the four columns drawn on it and the price side on the other face the other way up, and she stood in the middle of about nine hundred people at about the fifth hour with the chalk out and did not put anything on it, and a woman at a pitch of needles and thread looked at the slate and then at her and said the thing that nobody says to her about her ear.

“**You have not written anything on that for an hour.**”

“**That is about a slate.**”

And the woman said, “**It is, and I have had one of my own for about eleven years and it is the only object in my house nobody else has ever been allowed to touch.**”

---

The thing she was in the market for was the sheet on the fourth board with the list of two hundred and forty things on it, and she got what she wanted off a man at a pitch of nothing in particular without asking a single question, which is the method and it took about nine seconds.

She asked him what the column was for. He told her. She asked him who fills it in. He told her. She asked him how often. He told her.

**The second column was a column of repeaters.**

Not a column of complaints. Not a column of protests. **A column for the name of the person who repeated a thing.**

The man at the pitch had a slip of his own out of his coat, because he had come to the market to hand in a slip, and he handed it over and let her read it, and she read it in about eleven seconds and then read it again because of the box.

> *Particulars of a repetition. District of Wendle and Ashmoor. [number]*
>
> *Day of the notice. The notice. The words as they were spoken. **The person who repeated them.** The place. The persons present.*

Six fields. And one of the six was for a hand and not for a mouth, and it was the only one of the six with a name in it, and the other five could be written by anybody who was in the room.

---

She stood in the middle of the market and worked out where she sat in it, and it took her about a minute and a half, and she has written down that it took a minute and a half and not four seconds, because it did.

> *Ninety-second. A market of about nine hundred. A slip with six fields on it and the fourth one is a name.*
>
> *Here is the thing. **A box is cheaper than a witness and a witness can refuse.** That is the whole of it and it has taken me eleven years and about nine counties to arrive at it from the other end, and a clerk in a hall four days behind me said a true sentence about a document in nine seconds a month ago and I was not listening, and this is the other half of what he said and I have been carrying his half since the sixty-second and thinking it was the whole.*
>
> *Everything I have ever done on a road needs a person standing in the open who has decided to say another person's own words back to them. That cannot be got back. That is why it is the only thing in this kingdom that has ever moved a line. **And there is a form in this kingdom with a box in it for the person who did it, and about four hundred people a year fill that box in, and the other five boxes on the slip can be filled in by anybody in the room, and a man has to sit down and write a name, and I do not know one thing about that man that is wrong.***

---

The man with the slip came back for it about a quarter of an hour later and she gave it back and he said:

“**You are not going to write your name on anything, are you.**”

“No.”

“**Good. And I want to tell you what I would have done, because you have read it and I have watched you read it twice, and I would have written *a woman with a slate* and I would have been right, and that is the trouble with the box. It is not wrong. It is just not a person, and a clerk is going to write *a woman with a slate* at the end of it, and then it is a thing that happened in a market.**”

He put the slip in his coat.

“**And the woman with the slate is going to be a woman with a slate in about four hundred hands in about four years and none of them will know which one and it will be no use to anybody at all, and that is not a threat, that is just what a box does.**”

---

**And then she rang it.**

Not at the board. In the middle of the market, at about the sixth hour, in front of about nine hundred people, on her own account, and she had not told him and she did not look at him and she has written down in her own hand that she did not look at him and is not going to explain it.

It is a handbell no wider than her palm across the crown, and the crown has been re-cut inside by a chisel about a sixteenth of an inch, with a riffle file mark at four points, and there is wax over an older signal still in it, and it weighs less than a pound and it is the loudest thing in that square for about nine seconds.

She rang it once.

**And then she said one thing, at ten feet, in the ordinary voice, to a man about nine feet off who had a slip of paper in his coat and had been standing there for a quarter of an hour.**

“**You have got a box for the person who repeats a thing and I am the person who repeats things, and I have been doing it on eleven years of road, and there are about four hundred of these a year and you are one of the people who fills them in, and I am telling you that in a market and not to a clerk, and I would like it noticed that I chose a market, and I would like it noticed that a market is where I am known and a counter is where I am not.**”

---

Nobody said anything for about four seconds, and then about nine hundred people in a market of about nine hundred people all of whom had seen a woman with a slate saying a thing back to somebody at ten feet for a week in a town four days behind them did the thing that markets do.

They looked at her.

**And that is the whole cost of the ninety-second and it took about nine seconds and she paid it deliberately and she has written down that she paid it on purpose and that she would do it again in about a minute and a half if she had to do it again, and she is not going to be told about it by anybody, including him, and he did not try.**

It is not a cart and it is not a hiding and it is not a rescue. It is a woman with a slate in a market of about nine hundred people in a town with a Crown store eleven miles off, and about nine hundred people now know that there is one of her and can describe her, and about forty of them will say it in a different town inside a year, and there is no form in this kingdom on which a person may be charged with being a woman with a slate.

---

He came over at about the seventh hour, and he had been about nine feet off her left shoulder for about a quarter of an hour, and what he said was:

“**You did not tell me.**”

“**No.**

“**Why not.**”

“**Because you would have said it would be a good idea and you would have been right and it would have been on the page before the second stroke, and I have had one of those in six weeks and I have watched what it cost, and I am not having it twice and not from you and not about me.**”

He did not argue with that and he did not improve it and he wrote it down, and the reason he wrote it down is the reason he has written down every refusal in six weeks, and she read it upside down from behind him about four seconds later and did not say anything about it.

Then he said: “**I am not going to thank you for it.**”

“**Good. And I am not going to be thanked and I am not going to explain it and I am not going to let it be a thing that was for anybody else. If anybody asks me in Sennet why I rang a bell in a market, the answer is that there was a man with a box and I did not want to be in it. That is the whole reason and it took me about four seconds to get it and I had it before the second stroke.**”

She wrote the last entry of the ninety-second at about the ninth hour of the evening, on a stone by the water with her boots off, in her own hand, with the hours, and she filled the first and second columns and left the third and the fourth, and the fourth has two lines in it now and she has not improved either of them and is not going to.

> *The ninety-second. **A box is cheaper than a witness and a witness can refuse.** Six fields on a slip, and four of them can be filled in by anybody in the room, and the fifth is *the words as they were spoken*, and the fourth is *the person who repeated them*, and it is the only one of the six that wants a name, and I have spent eleven years being the fourth one and I did not know there was a form.*
>
> *I have been thinking about this all afternoon and here is the whole of it and I am not going to make it longer than it is. **Everything I have ever done needs somebody to stand in the open in front of other people and say another person's words back to them, and that cannot be got back, and I have been proud of that for eleven years like it was a sort of weather. And a form is cheaper than I am. A man sits down and writes four words in a box and goes to his tea, and the thing I do takes a woman standing in a market saying a sentence in her own voice until somebody turns round, and the form costs a penny and I cost a market.** And the form is the better instrument and I have got to say so in my own hand, because I have been wrong about this for eleven years and I found out about four hours ago from a man who sells nothing in particular and who is right.*
>
> *And that is not the finding. The finding is the fourth field. **A form can count a thing that a person did. It cannot make anybody do it, and it cannot stop it, and it cannot be wrong, and it is cheaper.** And that is why the form wins, and it wins in every kingdom there has ever been, and it wins against me every time, and I have been beating people at markets for eleven years with an instrument that costs more and lasts less and I have been calling that a victory because I have not been in a room.*
>
> *Third column, ruled apart, and I am merging nothing: **I rang a bell.** Mine, in her bag, in a market of about nine hundred people, once, and it is a handbell and it is not certified for anything and it is the only thing I own that I have ever made any use of, and I rang it in front of nine hundred people on my own account and about nine of them will describe me in four counties inside a year. **And nobody has asked me why and nobody is going to, and the man standing nine feet off my left shoulder did not ask me why, and he wrote down that he was not going to thank me, and that is the nearest anybody has come in four counties and it is not close enough and it is not going to get closer.**
>
> *And nobody has asked me about my ear in ninety-two days. I have written that down twice now in this book and I am not going to write it a third time.*
>
> *Which office is it. **He has written that he has got a name for it and that it is the wrong shape, and he has not written the name, and I have not asked him for it, and that is the first thing in about two months that either of us has had and has not asked for, and I want it written down that I did not ask, because I have asked him for a thing every other day since the thirty-third and there is a first one and I know exactly which day it was and I am not going to improve the sentence about it either.** Sennet is three days up this road. **A box is going there too, and so is a form, and so is about nine hundred rooms, and I am going to walk into the middle of them with a slate and a bell and no ear, and that is not a plan, it is a road, and I have been on one for eleven years.**
