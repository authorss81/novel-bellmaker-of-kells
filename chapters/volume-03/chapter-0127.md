# Chapter 0127 — A Woman With A Bell And No Reason To Use It

It was the fourth hour of the hundred and thirteenth day and Tomas Vale was on a step by the river on the flat with the barrow above him on the bank and the tarpaulin over the cradle and a woman nine feet off his left shoulder working a rope.

The rope was nine feet long and it hung off the frame of a net-loft and it was worn shiny in three places where a hand goes, and she had it in both hands and she was not pulling on it.

He had known for two days that he wanted to ask her something.

**He had also known, since the ninety-second, in a market of about nine hundred people, in front of everybody, exactly what her cover was worth, and he had watched her pay it, and he had written down that he was not going to make a woman pay it twice for a thing about himself.**

So he sat on the step and he did the thing he has done every day for a hundred and thirteen days, which is nothing, on purpose, and he has written down that he did it, once.

---

## And then she said it, and he did not ask her for any part of it, and she was not talking about him.

She was talking about the rope.

“**That is nine feet of it and it is worn in three places and it is not worn anywhere else, and I have been looking at it since the first day I came up here, and I have never once asked you to take your hand off it, and you have not.**”

“**It is not mine.**”

“**I know it is not. It is the loft's and the loft is the loft's and the man who has the loft is the man who has the rope, and there is a piece of chalk in your coat for things that are yours and there is no piece of chalk in the world for a thing that is not.**”

She put the rope back up on the frame where it hangs, exactly where it was.

“**Do you know what I have been doing for eleven years.**”

“**You have been telling people what they said.**”

“**I have been saying things out loud. **In a market, in a lane, on a step, at a gate, in a room with two people in it. And I say a person's own sentence back to them at about ten feet in the ordinary voice, and then I stand about nine feet further off, and that is the whole instrument, and it has been the whole instrument for eleven years.**”

“**It moves about four in ten.**”

“**It moves about four in ten and it costs me the being in it every time, and I have made my peace with that and I am not going to talk about my own peace with a man on a step.**”

---

## And then she gave him the other two, and she gave them at ten feet, unasked, in the ordinary voice, and she was looking at the water.

“**A thing said out loud in a market is heard by the people standing there. **That is all it is. It cannot be produced afterwards by anybody, not by you, not by a clerk, not by a man in a room four counties off with a form. And it dies. Not quickly. It dies with the people, one at a time, over about nine years, and the day the last of them is gone there is nowhere in this kingdom anybody can go and find out that it was said.**”

“**Yes.**”

“**And a thing written down is the other thing entirely. A thing written down is read by every clerk who comes into that room after you, for forty years, and it can be produced against you by a stranger you have never met and will not meet, and it does not need you to be in the room. **You have been refusing the cheap one for about nine weeks because you would not put a document about somebody else's sentence in a coat, and you were right, and you have spent nine weeks refusing it and eleven days being handed the expensive one by a court.**”

He did not say anything.

“**I have known that for eleven years and I had it the other way round and you worked it out in a market on the hundred and second in front of about nine people and you did not know you had.**”

---

## And he said the third one himself, out loud, once, and she did not help him and it took him about nine minutes.

“**There is a third one.**”

“**There is.**”

“**It is in a head.**”

“**Say the rest of it and then I will tell you whether it is any use, and I am telling you now that it will not be.**”

“**It cannot be got from me by anybody who wants it. **Not a clerk, not a district, not a man with a column, not me, not you, not anybody. There is no box in this kingdom that a head goes into, and there is no form for asking one, and no office in this city has ever had one and there is not going to be one.**”

“**That is true and it is worth nothing.**”

“**It cannot be given to anybody. **I could say it out loud to you at ten feet this morning and it would be four inches in the air between us and it would be gone in about nine years with both of us, and I could not put it in a document, and I could not put it in a bell, and I could not put it in a bell if I had one that would take it.**”

He stopped there.

“**And it dies with me. That is the third thing about it and it is the only one of the three that is not about anybody else.**”

---

**And here is the part where the whole of a hundred and thirteen days comes to a place, and he wrote it down that night and it is the trade and not a feeling, and it is the first time in his life that the thing he is holding cannot be made into metal at all.**

> *Everything I make is a thing a man cannot edit. **A claim in metal cannot be added to, cannot be taken back, cannot have an hour put on it, and cannot be shown to anybody on a page.** That is the whole of why I make them and it has been the whole of why since I was nine, and there is not one hour in a hundred and thirteen days when I have not been able to look at a thing I have struck and know that it is going to be exactly what I said it was on the day I said it, in front of nine hundred people or four, and that neither of those is going to be true tomorrow.*
>
> *And I have got a piece of paper in a fold in a bundle on a rail that a man cannot edit in either. **It is eleven lines long and it is the truest thing I have ever read and it is in a fair hand in a room anybody may walk into, and the office that wrote it could have crossed out the duty and kept the outcome and nobody would ever have known that I had read it, and I could not have proved it in a market with nine hundred people in it, and there is nothing I can strike.***
>
> *So the third thing is the one I have no instrument for. **A thing in metal, a thing on paper, a thing in the air at ten feet.** Three, and I have two of them and one of the two is the court's, and the one that is mine is in a head, and a head is not a trade and it is not a thing anybody has ever asked anybody for and I have got one and I am not going to be able to make it into anything at all.*

---

## And he did not put a question on the slate, and the count is eight, and a thing that is not done is not a thing that has been done.

He had it under his arm the whole morning. The price side of it was on the other face and it was the other way up, and it had been the other way up since a slate in a yard in a highland town, and there were two lines in the fourth column and neither of them had improved.

**He did not put a question on it and he did not hand it to her and he did not say *are you going to be the second*, and he wrote down once that he had not, and then he wrote under it that he was not going to write it down again, because she had said in her own book on the hundred and second that the third time she wrote the not-asking she would be counting, and a woman who counts is a woman waiting to be owed, and he is not owed and neither is she.**

**The count is eight. It has been eight since the eighty-third. A question offered on a step and not asked is not a question that was asked, and there is not going to be a ninth one out of a thing about his mother.**

---

**And he wrote on the step, at about the seventh hour, the thing the chapter has to have on the page, and he wrote it in his own book and not in hers, and there is not one line of it in her hand anywhere in eleven weeks.**

> *Nobody in this city has asked her for a reason at any hour in a hundred and thirteen days. Not a woman at a pitch, not a man in a corridor, not a clerk with a lamp, not me. **I have not asked her about her ear once since we left Kells and I am not going to write one word about it again.**
>
> *And today I found out that I had a thing in my head that I wanted, and she was standing nine feet off my left shoulder with a rope in her hands and the answer in the same building, and the honest shape of it is that **I did not ask because I have not got a right to spend the only thing she has got on a thing that is about my mother.** A thing said at ten feet in the open costs her the being in it. A thing written down costs me forty years and a stranger. **A thing in a head costs her nothing at all, and it is the only one of the three that I cannot get from her, and I did not ask, and I have not asked at any hour in four counties and a city, and I am not going to start because I have been refused nowhere else in a hundred and thirteen days and this is the one that would be mine and not theirs.**
>
> *And I am not going to be thanked. **She has not done a thing today and she is not going to be thanked for not doing a thing, and the whole of what I have got out of this morning is that a woman who has been deaf for the rest of her life looked at a rope and told me the price of two things out of three, and the third one is mine, and she has not been asked for it and will not be.**
>
> *Her bell is in her bag. **She rang it once since Kells, in a market of about nine hundred people, on her own account, and it was not for me and it did not help me and nobody asked her why and nobody is going to.** It has not moved since and it is not going to move because of anything I have found out in a room with a lamp in it.

---

**And at about the ninth hour of the evening he was on a step at the foot of the top stair with the tarpaulin over the cradle and the four bolts made fast, and the sack rolled against the wheel, and they came up off the flat together and she was nine feet off his left shoulder the whole way and they did not say anything for about seven hundred feet of hill.**

He wrote the last entry of the hundred and thirteenth, and filled the first, second and third columns and left the fourth as it was.

> *The hundred and thirteenth. **Three things, and I have two of them and the third one is mine and there is no instrument for it in this kingdom.**
>
> *A thing in metal cannot be edited. **A thing on paper cannot be got back and it is read for forty years and it can be produced by a stranger.** A thing said at ten feet in the open cannot be produced by anybody and dies with the people who heard it, in about nine years, one at a time. **I have been refusing the second one for nine weeks for a reason I wrote out flat and I was right, and I have been handed the second one by a court anyway, in a fair hand, in a fold, on a rail, and it says the truest thing I have ever read and I cannot strike it and I cannot carry it and I cannot give it to anybody.**
>
> *Second column, and the thing I did not do. **I did not put a question on the slate and the count is eight.** It has been eight since the eighty-third. I have written that down once tonight and I am not going to write it down again, **because she said in her own book on the hundred and second that the third time she writes the not-asking she will be counting, and a woman who counts is a woman waiting to be owed, and I am not owed.** A question offered on a step and not asked is not a question that was asked.
>
> *And the reason, in four inches, and it is not a rule. **There is a thing in my head about my mother and it is the only evidence I will ever have of it, and if I say it out loud at ten feet then it is a thing two people know, and the price of that is hers, because I have got nothing to lose by her being in a room and she has got the only cover she has left and she paid for the last of it in a market of about nine hundred people in front of everybody on the ninety-second and I watched her do it.** I am not the man who prices it again. **I have not got a right to it, and I have not got a reason, and I am not going to make one up at ten feet — that is what I said in a market on the hundred and second and I am not going to unsay it now that I want something.**
>
> *Third column, ruled apart, and the finding neither of us improved. **A head is the third thing and it is the only one that cannot be taken off me by anybody who wants it, and it cannot be given to anybody, and it dies with me.** It is not a trade. There is no craft in it and there is no clause about it and there is no plate in a printer's room two streets off that cuts it. **A man can be asked for a paper nine times and a woman can be made to say a thing at ten feet once, and neither of those can be done to this, and the price of it is that it is worth exactly nothing to anybody else including me.**
>
> *All five clauses stand. **Nothing rung, by me or by her.** Four bolts, and the frame has not been in a building since the thirty-third and it has been thirty feet off me in the open all day, and there is a loft in this city with a rope nine feet long in it that is not mine and that I have not touched in a hundred and thirteen days and have not asked about twice. The carrier in the sack untouched, thirty-six days, and I do not know where either of my two public ringings went and I am not going to find out. My own bell is in a sealed yard in Kells. **Thirteen documents in a coat, and eleven lines in a head, and those are two different objects and neither of them is a fourteenth.**
>
> *Which office is it. **It is a rail, a bundle, forty sheets, a string, and about forty people a week through a door, and the only document in this kingdom that says anything about my mother is eleven lines in a fair hand that any of them can read and none of them will.** And the only copy that exists is in a man who cannot put it in a coat, cannot put it in a bell, cannot put it on a board, and cannot say it out loud to four hundred people in a market. **I am not going to be the second of three and I am not going to ask, and there is a court on the middle terrace that will write a true thing down about the man standing in front of it for fourpence, and that is a different instrument and I have not decided anything about it and I am not going to tonight.**
