# Volume and Batch Index

**This file exists because summary filenames do not match batch numbers.** `state/batch-000N-summary.md` is the **global** batch number, counted across the whole novel. It is **not** the batch number inside a volume. Picking a predecessor summary by filename will pick the wrong file: **`state/batch-0005-summary.md` is Volume 01 Batch 0005, and Volume 02 Batch 0005 is `state/batch-0010-summary.md`.**

**Always resolve a predecessor summary through the table below, never by arithmetic on the filename.**

## The load set

Read these five. Everything else in `state/` is history and should be opened only when a specific fact is needed and its location is known.

| file | what it is | size class |
|---|---|---|
| `state/current.md` | **immediate state only** — where the manuscript is, object custody, the document register, ground, power, the two house rules | short |
| `state/continuity.md` | **the binding canon** — the one clock, the rules, the boundaries, the do-not-age list, the canon corrections | the one long file |
| `state/character-state.md` | who everybody is and what this volume did to them | short |
| `state/open-threads.md` | the live threads, and only the live ones | short |
| `state/volume-index.md` | this file | short |

`state/chapter-summaries.md` is **archive tier and is not in any batch prompt's read list.** Per-chapter detail for a batch is in that batch's summary. `state/batch-000N-summary.md` is the record of how a batch went and is read one at a time, not all ten together.

## One volume arc per line

- **Volume 01 — The Bell Before the Crime (Ch 1–50, Days 0–33).** A Crown survey carrier arrives with its clapper missing. A ward answers a form out loud in four columns. The archive burns, the yard is sealed, and Tomas Vale is taken out of his own lane. A district withdraws a finding, a captain releases a bell in a cradle that cannot be rung, and a bellmaker leaves Kells before dawn with a question he cannot answer.
- **Volume 02 — The Unpaid Names (Ch 51–100, Days 34–86).** A man with a barrow walks four counties with a list that cannot be unrolled. Nine pitches come off a market and a child goes on a cart. A man with no standing says one true sentence in a room and it costs a household a row. A practice is stopped in one district in writing. A room, a bench and the only copy of a form are taken lawfully under a clause he asked for. He rings a bell he did not make in a market square, apologises to about nine hundred people, and walks out of a town of two thousand with a barrow and no place.
- **Volume 04 — The Map of Owed Roads (Ch 151–200, Days 137–186).** A man who cannot read a map walks nine miles of road with a barrow he cannot pay for and finds out that the map was never going to tell him where to walk. A schedule is a drawing of a road and a road is a fact about the people who walk it, and this kingdom has one word for both of them. A woman at a pitch names the reason a man may not show a stranger a drawing before he has said it himself. A notice-reader with a book and no office tells him something he had not asked for and refuses him in one sentence. A smith refuses a theory four seconds before he can take one and says no twice. A woman with about nine hundred names in a book answers about a road and nothing else. Eleven people asked, two yes, nine no, and he does not argue with one of the nine. A river canton's bench will not carry a sentence anybody cannot certify. **Is the road to the coach inn open — and the answer is yes, and it has been for years, and it is not on any sheet anybody in the room has.** A man born at the end of the road gives four names for nine miles and has never been asked. **A schedule is a picture, a true sentence is the smallest thing anybody can carry, and a route is a person on foot and it costs a day.** *Batch 0001 paid one promise and refused to inflate it: a road can be walked, and no road was found, restored, registered or explained.*
- **Volume 04 Batch 0002 — A bypass, and what it is for (Ch 161–170, Days 147–156).** A barrow is one reason for a man to stop and he has been calling that a road for ten days. **A bypass takes the traffic and it takes the man who used to come past, and the households above the cut are the ones that lose a voice, and a woman at a door under the bank put a board over her own soft place and moved her own gate and said nobody had done anything wrong.** A district is right about a form's four boxes and wrong about the standing, and in this county the procedure is the whole of it. A man has mended his own work once and will not do it again, because a mend nobody can see is a mend somebody turns up one morning with a paper in their hand, and there is no counter in this kingdom where a man may say so — **and he said not one word about a bypass, and nobody thanked him.** Four people on a road agree about exactly one thing, and the one thing is a bridge, **because a bridge is the only thing a man can check himself and a person will agree with a plank about as far as a plank.** **A true sentence is the smallest thing anybody can carry, and he has four people and not one sentence, and the one man who would have carried one wanted a form and could not have it.** And a street of about forty houses with a fire going in it that nobody is hiding from anything, three words for it, and a man who cannot read a schedule and therefore cannot prove which of the two words is the one on the paper, in either direction, about that street or about a rectangle. *Batch 0002 paid eight things and inflated none of them: no road was found, restored, registered or explained, the rectangle was made smaller and not larger, and the chapter 0145 seed was not carried a third time.*
- **Volume 04 Batch 0003 — The line of settlements (Ch 171–180, Days 157–166).** **THE MIDPOINT REVERSAL, EARNED AT CHAPTER 0176 AND PAID.** Nine places go up the water, each with a market or a ford or a landing, each with people living at it, and each entered in a district's own fair hand in a return about a thing in a ward as work with a figure of mouths against it, paid coin and meal — and no box anywhere in four years of that clerk's book that says a household. **The reason arrives in a clerk's mouth while he rules a line, and he is right and he is not sorry and nobody thanked him: a house that is answered for is a house with a keeper, and a keeper is a man who can be asked to hold a line at the hour, and he cannot get keepers up that water.** Nobody is wicked, nobody is hidden, nothing was burned, nothing was forgotten, and no new enemy was named. A bridge the four agreed about in the last batch cannot be bought: two stringers and fourteen boards in a yard past the turning, and the man will not sell one board and wants the lot or none. One true sentence put once to four people and refused four times, in four trades' grammars, because a document can be carried by a district for forty years and a sentence dies with the last ear that heard it. A question asked about the road, one word back, a no accepted and walked past anyway, and nobody asked her for a reason at any hour including him. A fifth walker for one day and four again. *Batch 0003 paid the volume's finding and inflated none of it: the map was not read, the rectangle was not explained, no name was entered anywhere, the register stayed at thirteen, no bell was rung by anybody, and the chapter 0145 seed stayed at two. Its prose is a third of the length of the batch before it, and the post-batch review pass at `state/continuity.md` §10o records that as the one defect a repair pass may not rewrite.*

- **Volume 03 — The Quiet Office (Ch 101–150, Days 87–136).** A bell on a post above a notice-board rings with nobody near it, and the sheet under it is dated four days forward, and the whole of a coercion is arithmetic. A smith says a store in four seconds and refuses a theory. A bell is on a roll with an empty keeper's box. A leaf on a public board confirms a sister's hand and refuses to answer, and it is not taken. A woman with a slate finds a form with a box for the person who repeated a thing, and rings her own bell once in a market of about nine hundred people. Three sheets go on a trestle and a clause of a larger Renewal operation is read out to four hundred people. A notice in his own name goes on a board because a man with no standing cannot be served a document. A clerk at a counter names the second kind of paper. **A format has no door, and ninety-six days of learning to ask a person end at a building there is nothing in to ask. Then a city: the flat and its waiting bells, a counter that takes papers and not people, a court that publishes, a wire nobody can blame, a store with a column nobody will fill in, a hearing held off the page, a public record of it that says the person named produced nothing, a clerk who put his own hand on a public wall and lost four days, and three people in three yards who each said one true thing and were not thanked. And a man who has got one of the three answers, and not two, and not three: *if there were three of me.* Then a number that goes somewhere: a store's open book and a shut wall, a district he has never been in, a court's list of about nine hundred matters in the same five columns with the same empty fifth, a record and a file that are two different subjects, and a plate cutting about nine hundred forms a year with one four-inch box empty on one of them and no line through it. **An edited record of his mother's trial, and eleven lines in her own words saying she found that answers were being copied and that she took nine things and hid them, and a duty kept where a conviction should be, and no sentence at all about what became of her, and the proof of the edit being a form anybody in a room could see.** A sister's standing instruction on a public board before they have ever met, and four lines of it about a brother she has not asked him about. **A true sentence of his own sworn for fourpence in about nine seconds, and about two hundred people reading a copy of it in a market while a woman with a tray of pins worked along a board, and a rule with no face on it that put his name there.** And no stage, and no sense, and a man who has proved an edit and lost the only sentence anybody had ever said about him, and *if there were three of me*, written again and acted on by nobody.
