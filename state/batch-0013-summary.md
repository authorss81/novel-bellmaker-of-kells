## What the batch was for, and what it actually is

**The store of fittings, and his mother's trial, and a document, an edit and a duty.** The batch's job was the volume's midpoint: **he goes into a room he has never been in, not because he is a son but because a court publishes and a court form has no box for a reason and he did not need one; and he finds about eleven lines in his mother's own voice saying that she discovered that answers were being copied and that she accepted responsibility for hiding the first Crown clappers, and saying nothing whatever about whether she was imprisoned, killed, or used as an anchor.**

## The live state at the end of the batch, in one paragraph

Tomas Vale is on a step at the foot of the top stair of a city of about nine thousand people at about the ninth hour of the evening on Day 116, twenty-one days into the city, with a barrow, a frame in a cradle four feet off him with four bolts made fast and no clapper, a plain carrier rolled and against the barrow unstruck and thirty-nine days untouched, thirteen documents none of which is about him, **a public record of his own hearing that says the person named produced nothing, and a second document of his own that says something and is nailed to a board in a market, a store line in his head and not on a page, about nine hundred empty cells in a column he cannot fill and about nine hundred more on a list in another room ruled the same way, one empty fourth box in one form out of about nine hundred and nobody looking at it, eleven lines in a dead woman's own words in his head and nobody else's hands, a woman ten feet off his left shoulder who has not been asked for anything, a sister who has published an instruction he did not ask for, a market keeper four feet off him who taught him the price of a name and has not been told, a plate in a printer's room two streets off, a clerk at a desk who said *it is the same form* and went back to his tray, a rule with no face on it that put his name on a board, and the sentence the next volume opens on, written again and acted on by nobody: **if there were three of me.**

## What Batch 0004 has to carry, and it is five things

1. **The Office copies him — which is already in motion, because a district is required to circulate a sworn answer and a fair copy of his four sentences went out of a room with a lamp in it on a cart at about the sixth hour of Day 116.** About two hundred people have read it. There is no form by which a man who has been named on a board can be unnamed. **A person who is right is arrested on his account, and nobody in this kingdom is wicked, and that is the whole difficulty.**
2. **Eleven lines in his head and nowhere else, and the edit proved by a form, and no party standing, and no copy, and a court that does not give copies of a record of a matter to a person who is not a party to it.** He proved the strongest fact he has ever held and he cannot use it and Batch 0004 has to decide what a man does with a proof he may not file.
3. **Mara chooses once and is not thanked for it, and the three-person arrangement begins as a negotiation and not a reunion.**
4. **The plain carrier, unstruck at thirty-nine days, and a route map he has not got yet.** Both are the volume's forward pull and neither may travel early.
5. **The sentence *if there were three of me*, which is the sentence Volume 04 opens on, and it may be earned in Batch 0004 and it may not be resolved in this volume.**
