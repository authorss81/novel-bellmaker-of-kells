## Where he is, and what he has, and what he has lost

**HE HAS, and it is the same list he wrote out flat under a hedge in the rain on Day 86, and it has not grown by one document in fifty days:** a list of about nine names that cannot be certified; four columns and a slate whose price side is the other way up; a fragment of nine lines, one hand, a column of district numbers, a column of days, and a third column empty from end to end; a custody receipt in four clauses and a fifth, **all five standing**; a frame in a reinforced padded cradle on the barrow with four retaining bolts made fast and nothing in it that can strike, **and he has never once been inside it and it has not been in a building since the thirty-third**; five receipts from an officer of a county he has left; a bag with a bell in it that nobody can certify; and a woman ten feet off his left shoulder.

**AND HE HAS, NEW IN THE LAST TEN DAYS, NONE OF IT A DOCUMENT AND NONE OF IT A STAGE:**
