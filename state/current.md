## Where he is, and what he has, and what he has lost

**Tomas Vale is on a road down off the water from Miremarket on Day 86, in the rain, with a barrow.** He came into the town on the evening of Day 57 and leaves on Day 86: **twenty-nine days.** He is a man with a bar on his own bell, a receipt in five clauses, and a road. **He is not a man with a place, and he has stopped looking for one, and he says so on the last page, and he did not decide to stop and did not notice.**

**HE HAS, on the last page of his own book, in his own hand, and this is the inventory and it is the whole of what six weeks in this county produced:** a list of about nine names that cannot be certified at all, in four counties, that cannot be unrolled, made in about nine minutes a year by about eleven thousand people who are not clerks, and **there is no form for a list and there never has been**; four columns and a slate whose price side is the other way up; **a witness in a corridor who was not thanked and did not want to be**; **a fragment — nine lines, one hand, a column of district numbers, a column of days, and a third column empty from end to end**; a custody receipt in four clauses and a fifth, **all five standing, including on the morning it is inconvenient**; a frame in a reinforced padded cradle on the barrow with four retaining bolts made fast at every place it has been set down since the thirty-third, and no clapper in it, and he has never once been inside the cradle; **five receipts from an officer of this county, one for each sheet, in his own hand with the hour**; a bag with a bell in it that nobody can certify; and **a woman beside him who has said nine words on a landing on the seventy-second and will not be thanked for them.**

**HE HAS LOST, and none of it is refunded:** a room, a bench and five sheets. **A carbon that was the only copy of its form outside the building that holds the original.** The use for being right. The belief that a room over a draper's shop was his. The last person in four counties he could have been angry at.

**HE GAINED, and it is civic and it is unusable and it is not a stage:** that a careful life is a way of writing things down, and a document is what a person is read by, and a claim in metal is a thing a man cannot edit, and he has had one of those and **does not know where it went**. That a hall is not a person and never was, and a habit of carrying is the only thing he has ever been able to do about one. That a chore is a thing a man does for himself, and a firm is not a person. That a copy of a Crown sheet is Crown property from the moment it is made. That a certificate is a certificate of metal and a note and nothing else. That a district number is a filing reference and not a man. And **that the first of the two questions he came down the water with is answered and the second is not, and that the second is on the page as a third question and it is not a better one.**
