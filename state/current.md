## Where he is, and what he has, and what he has lost

**Tomas Vale is in Sennet on Day 96, at the foot of a stair on the top terrace of a city of about nine thousand people, with a barrow.** He came up the road from Miremarket on the eighty-seventh and arrived on the ninety-fifth: **about eight days and about seventy miles, and six days' road is what the distance is called by people who know it.** He has a bar on his own bell, a receipt in five clauses, a hearing in seven days he cannot answer, and a notice with his name on it nailed to a board in a market square four days behind him.

**HE HAS, and it is the same list he wrote out flat under a hedge in the rain on Day 86, and it has not grown by one item in ten days:** a list of about nine names that cannot be certified at all; four columns and a slate whose price side is the other way up; a witness in a corridor who was not thanked; a fragment of nine lines, one hand, a column of district numbers, a column of days, and a third column empty from end to end; a custody receipt in four clauses and a fifth, **all five standing**; a frame in a reinforced padded cradle on the barrow with four retaining bolts made fast and no clapper in it, and he has never once been inside it; five receipts from an officer of a county he has left; a bag with a bell in it that nobody can certify; and a woman ten feet off his left shoulder.

**AND HE HAS, NEW IN TEN DAYS, THREE THINGS THAT ARE NOT DOCUMENTS AND TWO THAT ARE THE OFFICE'S:**

- **A store.** A room with a counter, a book, a man and a shelf, in Ashmoor, eleven miles from a ford, and another four streets and four flights above his head in Sennet, **and a number at the top of the form.**
- **A description of the second kind of paper**, in a clerk's mouth at a counter at the foot of the top terrace: *a fitting, an instrument, or a box with a handle, issued out of a store against a district instruction with a number, and when it is fitted the answer is made for you, and the answer it makes is in your own voice and it is not a lie.* **He has not seen one. He has not seen a shelf. He says so in his own hand and refuses to write down the name of a fifth thing he has not seen.**
- **A printed slip and an unfilled form**, both the Office's, both in his coat. **He cannot fill the form in and has not tried and is not going to, and he has written down that carrying an unfilled form is not the same as a plan.**
- **A store number that is also a date.** He counted from the day at the foot of a board to the day at the top of it, got nine days, and checked it twice. It is the only thing on that board a man can check and he checked it.

**HE HAS LOST, and none of it is refunded:** the same list as on Day 86 — a room, a bench and five sheets; a carbon that was the only copy of its form outside the building that holds the original; the use for being right; the belief that carrying a room was a right; the last person in four counties he could have been angry at. **AND IN TEN DAYS HE HAS ADDED TWO THINGS TO THAT LIST AND BOTH ARE ONES HE CHOSE:** the last person in four counties he could have been angry at is still true, and now there is **nobody in this kingdom he can be angry at, and a schedule does not have a face, and that is not a comfort.**

**HE GAINED, and it is civic and it is unusable and it is not a stage:** that **a sound can be what a person is read by, and a sound is cheaper than a sheet and goes further, and the sheet is only there to say afterwards what the sound was**; that the Office's bells are not a conspiracy and not a villain and **a format, and a format has no door**; that a Crown repair comes out of a **store like any other store** and he has been asking *who* for ten days when the question is *where from*; that **a bell can be on a roll and the list of things is the one that matters and he has been carrying fright about the wrong object for two volumes**; that **there are two kinds of paper and he has been carrying one of them for fifty-six days and did not know it had a second leaf**; that **there is no box on the form for a question**; that **a man with no standing cannot be served a document and is therefore posted**, and that the same emptiness that stopped a form entering a household stopped a Crown sheet going into his hand; and that **a public board is the only surface in this kingdom with no standing attached to it**, which is why two people he is related to have used it and why he cannot.
