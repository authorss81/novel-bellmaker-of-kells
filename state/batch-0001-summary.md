# Batch 0001 Summary — Chapters 1–10

**Volume:** 01 — *The Bell Before the Crime*  
**Batch:** 0001  
**Status:** Complete

## Batch result

A fixed Crown survey carrier arrives at the Vale Bellhouse without its clapper and rings in the locked casting room. Tomas Vale preserves the carrier, finds a child-sized wax handprint, a damaged physical route, and a note attributed to Mara Vale, then traces separate witness accounts to a dawn Low Ward inspection on route L-4. The inspection was filed before the carrier sounded, making the warning plausible without proving who accepted responsibility or whether any local answer was copied.

Tamsin Rook recovers her stolen courier handbell, traces its altered wear, and keeps the object while offering only a supervised comparison. Sabin teaches Tomas the consent chain and the refusal test. Nell Fenn gives the private trial handbell one limited, informed answer; the binding costs Tomas a failed small casting, a split tuning file, and one episode of ringing ears. He reaches only the edge of Stage 1 and can sense that Nell's answer is strained, not identify its cause.

Oren records Tomas's Hush account, seals the fixed carrier in place, and later uses the narrow pending-hearing clause of a Crown holding writ. Halden and Oren sign a seven-day joint receipt placing only the fixed carrier in neutral watch custody while it remains in its mounting frame. The trial handbell and ordinary working bells stay at the Bellhouse under a written no-public-ringing order. Tomas refuses to turn one private answer into a town command.

## Immediate next obligation

Before the rescheduled dawn inspection, Tomas and Oren must test whether three independent local answers can carry a warning while preserving disagreement and refusal. The copied-answer mechanism, the Silent Muster's full purpose, the missing surveyor's identity, the child handprint's owner, Mara's route into the Quiet Office, and the altered handbell's wider route remain unresolved.

## Object custody after Chapter 10

- **Fixed stripped Crown survey carrier:** waist-high, fixed carrier, no clapper, child-sized wax handprint; in the Kells watch store in its mounting frame and reinforced padded cradle. The retaining bolts were released only for transfer. Crown ownership is unresolved. The trial binding is not on this object.
- **Tamsin's stolen courier handbell:** hand-sized, no wider than her palm, portable, altered wear in its crown where wax was laid over an older signal; recovered and retained by Tamsin. Tomas has read the wear once on the Bellhouse threshold without touching the bell. It is not the fixed carrier and does not bear its wax handprint.
- **Tomas's trial handbell:** newly cast from ordinary stock; bound to a sliver of the market-court L-4 posting wedged into its frame as witness matter, Tomas's narrow keeper duty, and Nell's one private answer. The paper never touches the rim and can be removed. It remains at the Bellhouse under the written no-public-ringing order and cannot be used for a second claim until answered or deliberately unmade.
- **Other loose relay bells:** remain at the Bellhouse under the no-public-ringing order; no broader seizure has occurred.

## Working day-count for Batch 0001

The L-4 notice was issued and posted three days before the carrier rang. The posted heading reads "dawn, seventh day from posting"; the inclusive counting rule is the market-court clerk's own addition on his public board in Chapter 6, not text on the posted notice. The original dawn therefore falls the morning after Chapter 10. Nell states in Chapter 5 that Jory was taken six days and Edan disappeared five days before she spoke, which places them on Day −5 and Day −4, both still before the notice. The Chapter 10 pending-hearing stay moves the inspection seven days, making the rescheduled dawn ten days after the carrier first rang. Chapters 11–20 must work inside that ten-day window. Halden gives Tomas the route number and the day in Chapter 2 but folds the order's filing record under his thumb; the precedence of the order is not established for Tomas until Chapter 5.
