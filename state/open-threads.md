## The two Volume 02 payoffs that were required and are NOT paid

- **Mara's trail. NOT MENTIONED IN ANY CHAPTER OF VOLUME 02.** `outline/volume-02.md`'s seventh mystery item required it to be confirmed to be hers and to refuse to answer, and the premise and the relationship progression both required her. **She appears in Volume 01 in Chapters 1, 4 and 8 only.** **No state file may report it paid.** The loose leaf is in Amos Trewin's stock book, four counties away, **is a leaf of unknown hand, and the one permitted opening was spent on the fifty-second and did not confirm itself** — older state files called it *Mara's leaf* and the prose never made that attribution. **She is load-bearing in `outline/ending.md` for Volumes 9 and 11.** See `state/continuity.md` §11.

## The three questions, and the state of each

1. **How does a Crown office turn one clerk's answer into a whole settlement's consent? — ANSWERED, Day 86, in his own hand.** *It does not need to. A roll does not need anybody's answer; a duty is a mouth; a mouth is for a claim; and the claim is made in a queue, in about nine minutes, in the ordinary voice, by about eleven thousand people a year who are not clerks and are not agents of anything.* **The answer is small and it is final for this volume and it is not a mechanism and it names nobody.**
2. **Where are names sent when they are registered? — NOT ANSWERED AND NOT TO BE ANSWERED IN VOLUME 02.** A district officer asked his district twice in writing and had no answer to either. A clerk of four years has never been upstairs and is not going to be. **On Day 82 a dyer with a key to his own upper rooms took nine lines out of a district's books in this town and the nine lines were numbers**, two of them the same number with two different days against them and one struck through with a day and nothing against the striking. **A district number is a filing reference and not a man**, and a reference going somewhere is not a person. **That is the whole of six weeks and it is a smaller thing than anybody was carrying and it is on the page.**
3. **Which office is it. — THE THIRD QUESTION, and it is on the page under a numeral and it is not a better one, and it is the question Volume 03 opens on.** It was asked by a woman with a slate from ten feet off his left shoulder on a road in the rain on Day 86, and he answered it in four words and did not improve it in the morning: **Nobody has told me.** **He did not write it down before she said it and he has written down that he did not.**

## The threads Volume 02 opened and Volume 02 did not close

- **A boy of eight is a name and not a place.** Taken in the round before the L-4 notice and not come back. **Not found, not placed, not named, not rescued and not brought back, and no chapter of Volume 02 finds him by a piece of paper.** He is in about nine thousand kitchens in four counties and there is no book of him anywhere in this kingdom and there never has been and there is no form for one. **Ashlade, four counties up, about eleven hundred people away, is a name in a line that has not been answered this year or the year before and there is nobody at that house now to answer it with, and no person is in Ashlade in any chapter from 51 to 100.**
- **The five dates in a drawer.** Six certified returns, one name, one hand, five different dates, and the man has not been in the house for six years. **UNACCOUNTED FOR. Touched once, in Chapter 96, on purpose, and refused: he had nine lines of numbers in his hand, he felt it start, and he wrote the sentence down again — a person who joins them has joined them because they are both dates. They are not a forgery, not a conspiracy, and not explained away, and they are open.**
- **Tamsin Rook's answer.** In a clerk's hall in Miremarket, in an auditor's own book, with her name at the head of it and the hour against it, since about the fifty-eighth. **He has known it was there since Chapter 96, where a dyer said the fire had been out in two rooms for a fortnight, and he has deliberately not asked for it, and the reason is his own and is five words: she did not ask me.** Nobody rescued her, nobody held it over her, nobody asked her for a reason, and nobody made her a spectacle. **It is hers and it cannot be taken out of that building by anybody, including her.**
- **The district's finding of the twenty-eighth.** Withdrawn on the thirty-third by a surveyor on her own paper. **It is not in Miremarket's clerk's hall and never was; it is in a building in another county, in a file, on a shelf, and there is a form for asking and the form is correct and about three hundred people could have used it in seven weeks and not one of them has.** **Tomas Vale has not asked for it and says why in his own hand, and a clerk told him he was not going to and told him why, and said it was not a virtue.**
- **A set of rolls on a shelf in a real building in Miremarket.** About eleven hundred lines a year into that hall; about eleven thousand a year across the district; **the answers are ours and the names go up.** A clerk of four years has never been upstairs, asked the question, said no twice, and asked for it to be written down that he said no.
- **A hundred yards between six households and a firm of nine people.** Six households were paid about fifty-one pounds on Day 79 and a firm of nine was answered and finished the same morning, **and the two things were about a hundred yards apart and neither of them went to the other, and only one person in that county could see both and he has no standing to say so to anybody.**
- **A practice stopped in one district in writing.** Worth the rest of this month and every month after it. **Not worth the ninth hour of the evening of the sixty-ninth.** About three hundred and sixty pounds a year is the nine districts that adopted it; there are nineteen under the county; and if all nineteen stopped it the figure would be about seven hundred and sixty pounds a year, **which is about the price of a small bridge and is not money anybody finds in a place anybody looks.**
- **A clerk who can be named.** **Elias Nabb was named in a market in front of about nine hundred people on Day 77, out loud, on purpose, in a list of four people who were right, and the market keeper put the price in Tomas Vale's own hearing: for about four days nobody in that hall will be able to shut a line, because a clerk can be named, and the notice on the wall means what it says.** **He has not been told, has not been asked, is not sorry, is not converted, and nobody is going to come and tell him it was worth it.**
- **An unnamed man who went up the water.** Four months before a line was shut in a street off the market; the woman at the door said so before the officer had his book open; the line was closed in the correct column; the only record in this kingdom that he was anywhere at all is a mark in a column; **a shut line is a man who has gone and answered, and so he is not lost. His name is not on the page, was not asked for, and nobody is going to look for him.**
- **A woman at a copper in Bramden, nine years and nine lines out.** Tomas promised not to visit her and has not. What she decides and what it costs is not on the page and is not this volume's to supply.
- **About nine hundred people who remember a thing a dead man said to them at a gate.** None of them can be paid, **a hand is a record and a memory is a story, and that is why he has not been robbed, and it is not a comfort and was not invented for a bag of money in a room.** The six who have a hand in a yard-book are paid. The rest are not owed anything and are not owed anything by anybody in this county.
- **Nine pitches off a market of about nine hundred.** Ock Randall to a lane; Jessa Vinch's butter bought on credit against a pitch that is not there; the Larkins at the end of a row that is not there; about four pounds a quarter off each of nine households over a season and about thirty-six pounds a quarter in a year when the season is what it is. **Nobody was rescued and nobody was told it was worth it.**
- **A child on a carrier's cart on Day 72.** Not found, not placed, not named, not rescued, not brought back. **It is for the name, that is all it is for and there is nobody at the end of it, and that was said by the mother in the open on a landing and is not undone anywhere in Volume 02.**
- **A household at the end of the east lane.** A book in a drawer that is not going to be asked about, nine columns, about nine years, and a woman carrying the whole of this volume in her head with nobody in the house who can read. **She is not afraid of a line. She is afraid of a cart, and there has not been a cart, and she has not got a reason and is not going to be given one.**
- **A woman who keeps about nine hundred names in a book and has never been asked what it is.** She wrote the hour down in her own hand on Day 82 and kept the sheet and would not give it to him, and said she would rather be a woman who has written one thing down than a woman who has been thanked. **Not owed a visit. There is no column for her and no form on which anything could be done for her at all.**
