Not torn and not cut: the paper is smooth and uncreased there, the column rules that rule the panels either side stop short and do not cross it, the ink either side is a slightly different black, the sheet has been folded and unfolded about nine hundred times and that is the one place on it that has not been folded through, and the numbers either side of it go on without a break. It is in the same place.** **He does not know whether that is a thing done to every copy or a thing done to the plate, did not find out in a corridor, wrote it down, and joined it to nothing — not to the empty fourth box on a form in a printer's room two streets off, not to the keeper's field ruled past the end of a line in a county he has left, because three empties in three counties is a man arranging a thing and he has had that rule since the ninetieth.** **THE ROAD SHEET'S FIFTH PANEL, THE PANEL FOR THE DISTRICT ABOVE THE COACH INN, HAS BEEN GONE FROM EVERY COPY IN THE SAME RECTANGLE SINCE DAY 82. 2. **WHY THE OLD NETWORK ACCEPTS A CROWN CLAPPER — SEEDED ONCE AND NOT CONCLUDED.** In Chapter 0145 a man stacking sacks in a yard, who is not a keeper and was not thinking about it, said that when he was a boy a line came up through the bottom of that wall and answered, and that there was a day it stopped answering, and that his father went looking for it and never told anybody what he found; and that the ones above him stopped too, and that when the ones above you stop the ones above them go quiet, and the last one out is always a building with a bell on it that has to go at the hour. **The chapter says on the page that it is a man talking about his father and not a finding, and Tomas writes down that he did not ask why a route that answers is a route that does not have to be argued with, that he has not come away with it and is not going to go and look for it. ## The nine threads Batch 0005 opened

3. **THE PUBLIC TRUTH BELL, AND WHAT A CLAIM IN METAL CANNOT DO.** He struck a bell he did not make, once, in a yard, in the ordinary way, and wanted to put four sentences in it and could not, because a man ringing a bell he did not make says whatever the bell's claim is and the claim in that bell was the hour. **The only instrument in this kingdom that cannot be edited is the one he used and the edit was not his, and afterwards there is no form in this kingdom that can tell the time off a bell that is telling you a true thing, and that absence is four inches long.** OPEN, and it is the reason a public claim in a city like this one cannot be made and it is the volume's last craft fact.
