## The approving return, and the practice it approves

**The practice:** where a household is returned as unable to make up its mind, **the office records one hand against the line and treats the line as answered**, and the return calls it a settlement of the difficulty, and the difficulty is a household. **The reason given is true**: a household is not a person, a schedule does not hold open, a second round is a cart and a man and a day, and a man will not come twice to one door. It says the household is informed and may come and dispute the line at any hour the office is open. **Every word of it is correct. The two things it does not say are that a household is informed by some means at some point, and that one hand does not know what the other four hands wanted.**

**This is the live contradiction the next batch must keep alive, and it is not resolved at the end of Chapter 70 and must not be:** Tomas's whole argument is that **an answer is a person and may be withdrawn**, and the approving return is a good document that says a line is a line in a schedule, and both of those are true, and the reader is not left sure Tomas is right. **Batch 0003's reversal at about Chapter 76 is the moment this stops being a matter of opinion: the ward's method is found to be correct and insufficient, and the roll does not need anybody's answer, it needs one signature and a stamp.** That belongs to Batch 0003 and not to this one, and nothing in Chapters 61–70 may anticipate it.

- **A hundred yards between six households and a firm of nine people.** Six households were paid about fifty-one pounds on Day 79 and a firm of nine was answered and finished the same morning, **and the two things were about a hundred yards apart and neither of them went to the other, and only one person in that county could see both and he has no standing to say so to anybody.**
- **A practice stopped in one district in writing.** Worth the rest of this month and every month after it. **Not worth the ninth hour of the evening of the sixty-ninth.** About three hundred and sixty pounds a year is the nine districts that adopted it; there are nineteen under the county; and if all nineteen stopped it the figure would be about seven hundred and sixty pounds a year, which is about the price of a small bridge and is not money anybody finds in a place anybody looks.
- **A clerk who can be named.** **Elias Nabb was named in a market in front of about nine hundred people on Day 77, out loud, on purpose, in a list of four people who were right, and the market keeper put the price in Tomas Vale's own hearing: for about four days nobody in that hall will be able to shut a line, because a clerk can be named, and the notice on the wall means what it says.** **He has not been told, has not been asked, is not sorry, is not converted, and nobody is going to come and tell him it was worth it.**
- **An unnamed man who went up the water.** Four months before a line was shut in a street off the market; the woman at the door said so before the officer had his book open; the line was closed in the correct column; the only record in this kingdom that he was anywhere at all is a mark in a column; **a shut line is a man who has gone and answered, and so he is not lost. His name is not on the page, was not asked for, and nobody is going to look for him.**
- **A woman at a copper in Bramden, nine years and nine lines out.** Tomas promised not to visit her and has not. What she decides and what it costs is not on the page and is not this volume's to supply.
- **About nine hundred people who remember a thing a dead man said to them at a gate.** None of them can be paid, **a hand is a record and a memory is a story, and that is why he has not been robbed, and it is not a comfort and was not invented for a bag of money in a room.** The six who have a hand in a yard-book are paid. The rest are not owed anything and are not owed anything by anybody in this county.
- **Nine pitches off a market of about nine hundred.** Ock Randall to a lane; Jessa Vinch's butter bought on credit against a pitch that is not there; the Larkins at the end of a row that is not there; about four pounds a quarter off each of nine households over a season and about thirty-six pounds a quarter in a year when the season is what it is. **Nobody was rescued and nobody was told it was worth it.**
- **A child on a carrier's cart on Day 72.** Not found, not placed, not named, not rescued, not brought back. **It is for the name, that is all it is for and there is nobody at the end of it, and that was said by the mother in the open on a landing and is not undone anywhere in Volume 02.**
- **A household at the end of the east lane.** A book in a drawer that is not going to be asked about, nine columns, about nine years, and a woman carrying the whole of this volume in her head with nobody in the house who can read. **She is not afraid of a line. She is afraid of a cart, and there has not been a cart, and she has not got a reason and is not going to be given one.**
- **A woman who keeps about nine hundred names in a book and has never been asked what it is.** She wrote the hour down in her own hand on Day 82 and kept the sheet and would not give it to him, and said she would rather be a woman who has written one thing down than a woman who has been thanked. **Not owed a visit. There is no column for her and no form on which anything could be done for her at all.**

- **A practice is stopped in one district in writing.** It holds for the rest of this month and every month after it.
- **A firm of nine people has a hand on its own line and a yard that is gone in the spring.**
- **About fifty-one pounds has been paid into six hands.**
- **A room over a draper's shop in Miremarket has been emptied of five sheets and a bench, lawfully, and a draper's man will not let a room to a man with a barrow again, and says so in one sentence and is not unkind about it.**
- **About nine hundred people in a market have heard a clerk's name.**
- **A queue in a market has learned to say a claim back and does not know it is doing anything, and about a hundred people did it on Day 83.**
- **A Bellwrights' Measure room has found out that the Measure is two trades inside one word, and a master of the Measure has said both halves in one afternoon and not seen them, and the split is not settled and is not a defeat and is not a thing anybody in it is a villain about.**
