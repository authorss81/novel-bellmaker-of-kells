Her four columns stand, the fourth has two lines, the price side is the other way up, and **on Day 134 four lines in her own hand went into the apron of a woman who lets a room by the hour and he did not ask what they said and does not have them and is not going to.** Neither of them says what the road is for this time.
- **Tomas and Mara: three payments and no resolution.** A leaf he did not take, a building he did not enter, a standing instruction he did not go under, a row he did not read, a board he did not come back to, **and a room he was held in on Day 133 in a building she was inside and did not come to.** She chose a record over a man and he is not thanked and does not thank her and does not know what she did and is not going to find out, **and it is not a reconciliation and nothing between them is resolved and she is not a thing he can summon.** A district's paper on the top terrace has a woman's name at the head of it and he still has not decided and is not going to.
- **Tomas and the bell he did not ring, the volume's standing refusal, which is now also a thing he did.** **A claim in metal cannot be edited, and the only instrument in this kingdom that could carry a true sentence out of him in one evening and put it in front of every door in this city is a sound, and a sound in a city of about nine thousand people is the hour.** He struck a bell he did not make, once, in a yard, in the ordinary way, on the evening of the hundred and thirtieth, and about nine minutes later a form four floors up had a day and a number and a person in it, **and he wanted to put four sentences in that bell and could not, and the word in that bell was the hour, and afterwards there is no form in this kingdom that can tell which of the two a man had done.** He rang nothing else. No bell he has ever made is unbarred.
- **Tomas and the people who are right: THIRTY-FOUR NOW — twenty-three through the five batches of Volume 03, and ELEVEN MORE IN VOLUME 04 BATCH 0001, none of whom is a door, none of whom wanted anything and none of whom is thanked: a woman at a pitch who named the reason a man may not show a stranger a drawing; a notice-reader with a book and no office who told him something he had not asked for; a smith who refused a theory four seconds before he could take one and said no twice; a woman with about nine hundred names in a book who will answer about a road and nothing else; a hauler who answered in four seconds and would have been embarrassed if he had been thanked; a woman with a ford tally; a clerk of a bench who asked him the first question anybody in that room asked anybody; a bench of nine people who would not carry a sentence nobody could certify; a woman with a hire book who would not say a date out loud in a room; and a man born at the end of a road who had never been asked what it is called.**

- **Tomas and the people who are right: twenty-three now, in five batches, four counties and a city, none of whom is a door, none of whom wanted anything, and one of whom cost himself about four days and a hundred yards of corridor on a public wall and will never be told it was worth it, on Tomas's account.** From this batch: a district round who said there is nothing on this to detain anybody with; a storekeeper who told him he would like him to leave; a clerk who said a person who has been entered in a column once does not get entered twice for nothing; an arch clerk who said a notice comes off a board when it is done; a man stacking sacks who said he has not asked and is not going to; a clerk who strikes four times on somebody else's paper and does not read it; a man in a chair four days' road away who read a charge and would not be told what it means and told him so; and a woman who shuts a window with four lines in her apron and shut it.
- **The volume's best civic act is a woman inside a building who chose a record over a man, on her own account, unasked, and was not met and was not thanked, and the man it was about is not going to find out what she did.** **The volume's worst civic act is still his, and it is a sound in a yard, and it is the best thing he has ever done, and both of those are true at once and he writes them down in that order.**
