### Chapter 0041 — What a Page Is For

- **POV/location/time:** Tomas; the ward board, the market court and the Bellhouse yard, **Day 22**, full day.
- **Goal:** Hold the ward's public record together in the twenty-four hours after a round in which a child was taken lawfully and their own procedure worked, and find out what the round cost the practice.
- **Resistance:** about a hundred and thirty people are frightened and three hundred and forty came out in the dark and one household is on the floor; **a ward that shows up is a ward that is noted, and Rudd's objection is on the record and is true**; the crown of the Crown's form is not the practice, it is the witness column, and the *not returned* / *accompanying* alignment is still private to Nell and is not to be spent as an accident; **Tomas is inside four days of silence and the ward wants him to explain the market lane**; Hask's men are back at the low end of the turn.
- **Turn/reveal:** the round's own numbers, read from Collis's return as it reaches the ward, show the practice working and show the practice not stopping anything, and Tomas has to hold both in one column without merging them. The twenty-two doors with an objection in a margin are a real gain and the child's removal happened anyway, and **the only honest sentence available is that a procedure can make an office legible and cannot make it stop** — which is Crake's sentence and Tomas may not improve on it.
- **Decision/consequence:** Tomas writes the day's record with the same three columns and hands the writing to somebody other than himself. **He does not ring anything and does not say what to do about the children, and on the Day 24 the ward will still not have a position.** Someone else does the thing Tomas would have done, and it is not his version.
- **Power/resource:** No new power. Tomas gains a fact and loses the belief that his page is the town's instrument.
- **Ending pull:** the Crown's answer to the round is not a notice about the round. It is a form.

### Chapter 0042 — The Name in the Column
