## The form of the chapter, because the last batch is the evidence and it is not an instruction to imitate

**No `##` section heading in any chapter. Not one.** A heading that states the finding, the price or the shape of the section it sits over is a beat sheet, and a chapter that reads as an outline with scenes attached to it is a defect. A `---` rule between scenes is the house style and is welcome.

### 1. LENGTH, AND THE FIGURE THAT SETS THE FLOOR

**MEASURED ACROSS THE THREE BATCHES OF VOLUME 04, whitespace word count of the whole file: Batch 0001 mean 3,085, minimum 2,140, maximum 3,979. Batch 0002 mean 2,695, minimum 2,158, maximum 2,951. BATCH 0003 MEAN 829, MINIMUM 686, MAXIMUM 1,099, AND ITS TOTAL IS 8,289 AGAINST 26,948 AND 30,847.** **That is a third of the length of the batch before it and it is the finding §10o records as unfixable by editing, because a chapter that is short because it is a beat sheet cannot be lengthened without being rewritten, and the batch is not to be restarted.** **It is entirely fixable in THIS batch, and the floor is therefore binding: NO CHAPTER UNDER 1,800 WORDS, THE BAND IS 2,000 TO 3,000, AND YOU STATE THE FIGURE YOU HIT FOR EVERY CHAPTER AND THE MEAN IN YOUR OWN SUMMARY.** **A chapter earns its length with work, dialogue, consequence and interiority, not with a longer day-book, and a day-book is not a chapter.** If a scene is finished earlier than 2,000 words, the day had more in it than you have written, and the answer is a second scene and not a longer recital.

### 2. SHAPE, AND THE TEMPLATE THAT TEN CHAPTERS IN A ROW FELL INTO

**Batch 0003 ran one shape ten times: the fourth hour and the four bolts, a refrain paragraph about the frame not being in a building, a second scene with one worker encounter, two `---` breaks, and a five-entry day-book opening at the ninth hour of the evening. Ten of ten.** The breaks recorded in the clock — no seventh hour in four chapters, no fifth hour in one — are **subtractions inside the same skeleton and not a variation, and a shorter hour list is not a different chapter.** **Four rules, and all four are checkable against the finished files:**

- **The frame-and-four-bolts opening opens NO MORE THAN THREE OF THE TEN.** Where it is not the opener, the fact that the frame has not been in a building since the thirty-third is still on the page or in the day-book of that chapter, **in different words each time, because a fact repeated in the same words in ten files is a refrain and a refrain is a drum.**
- **THE SCENE COUNT VARIES AND THE VARIATION IS IN THE PROSE.** At least three chapters with a single break and at least three with three or more. **Ten chapters of two scenes each is ten chapters of the same chapter.**
- **AT LEAST THREE CHAPTERS RUN MOST OF THEIR LENGTH ON ONE SUSTAINED EXCHANGE** between two people, with the work visible and the theme nowhere stated aloud. A chapter that is a series of arrivals and departures is a roll call with weather.
- **AT LEAST TWO CHAPTERS OPEN ON A PERSON WHO IS NOT TOMAS**, inside that person's own work, and Tomas comes into the chapter rather than standing at the edge of it being handed the day.

### 3. THE ROLL-CALL QUOTA IS BANNED, AND IT IS THE THIRD VERSION OF THE SAME DEFECT

**§10n finding 6 named one voice for every character. Batch 0003 replaced it with a per-chapter quota: in Chapters 0172, 0175 and 0179 each of Pell, Mab, Isa and Ned gets exactly one interchangeable line stating his trade function — *Pell counted the heap the way he counted loads*, *Mab looked at the cut… I am not counting water* — and any one of them can be cut and the chapter is unchanged.** **A LINE THAT CAN BE DELETED WITHOUT CHANGING THE CHAPTER IS NOT A CHARACTER AND IS NOT ALLOWED.** So: **every one of the four who is on the page in a chapter has one thing of their own that is not the theme and not their trade label** — a debt, a grievance, a place they have to be, a thing they were told and did not do, a thing they want by the fourth hour. **And at least three of the ten chapters must have one of the four off the page entirely**, because four people who turn up in every chapter are a chorus and not a road. **A second mouth is not a way of stating the theme. If a character can be removed and the theme still lands, the character is a mouthpiece and is cut or given something of their own to want that is not the theme.** Tomas is the only character permitted the long sub-clause, and even he earns it.

### 4. THE DAY-BOOK, ITS CEILING, AND WHAT IT MAY NOT BE

**A day-book is a man's own book, in his own hand, and it must RE-DERIVE and not RE-PRINT.** *A motif or a ritual sentence may be carried forward word for word — the* Which office is it *closing paragraph, the fifth clause's wording wherever the clause is named, the custody formula, the fourth clause of the three-person arrangement, the arranging-is-the-damage sentence, the four sentences of a bell's claim, and a true sentence said in a bench room and written in its own day-book because the rule it states IS the fourth clause.*

**MEASURE YOURS ON THREE TESTS AND NOT ONE, because the single test this project ran for four volumes is structurally blind to two whole classes of defect.** **Test one is the §10c test: the longest common word-run between any two paragraphs in the same file. Test two is the line-level near-duplicate scan: the longest common word-run between any two lines of the same file carrying twenty-five words or more, which is the only test that can see a blockquote entry printed twice on adjacent lines inside itself — that is how Chapter 0168 stood at a hundred and ten duplicated words through a whole batch and through its own certified sweep. Test three is the cross-file run over every pair of your ten chapters, which is the only test that can see a sentence the batch carries on purpose in all ten.** **A run goes on your repair list if it is at or over the standing figure OR if it re-prints the beat the chapter is built on, AT ANY LENGTH. A central beat is not measured by its length, and a file that reports one number has not reported the file.**

**THE EXEMPTION HAS A BUDGET AND A MEASUREMENT, because §10n's rule is that an exemption without a measurement is a hidden repetition, and Batch 0003 grew one anyway.** Batch 0003's exempt text, named here with its figure so you inherit the measurement and not the habit: **a 131-WORD CUSTODY BLOCK carried word for word in all ten chapters, varying only in the strike count; a 24-word register block in nine of the ten; the 4-word *Which office is it* opener in all ten; and a 27-to-31-word refrain paragraph in all ten. That is about 187 words a file, 1,874 of the batch's 8,289, twenty-two per cent.** **Your budget: the batch's text carried near-identically across all ten files may not exceed 190 words a file, and the custody block may not grow past 131 words without a reason you can name in one sentence. AND YOU MUST PUBLISH THE FIGURE, PER FILE AND FOR THE BATCH, IN YOUR OWN SUMMARY, WITH ALL THREE TESTS IN THREE SEPARATE COLUMNS.**

**THE SIZE OF IT. Batch 0002's day-book was forty-seven per cent of the batch and seventy-one per cent of the batch's words sat inside bold markers. Batch 0003's day-book was thirty-four per cent, and that is better, and thirty-four per cent of a chapter that should have been 2,700 words is still four short recitals instead of one scene. BRING THE DAY-BOOK DOWN FURTHER — no chapter's day-book may exceed thirty-five per cent of that chapter and the batch's day-book may not exceed thirty per cent of the batch — AND STATE THE FIGURES YOU HIT. This is not an instruction to write less, it is an instruction to notice what you are doing, because a day-book that has become the book rather than the man's account of the day is the failure this volume walked into twice.**

**THE THIRD COLUMN IS A RULING ON THE DAY AND NOT A PARAGRAPH OF THE PROSE AGAIN.** **Every chapter of Batch 0002 stated its finding three times — in the prose, again in the third column of its own day-book, and again in the closing paragraph of that same day-book — and the sentence-level rule was satisfied and the repetition happened at the level of the chapter, which is the level the rule does not measure.** **State a thing the number of times the scene needs it and not once more.**

**A man in a day-book counts things because he is a maker and cannot help it, and the round numbers are not one metronome for every man.** *About four seconds* belongs to the Office and to a plate and to a refusal. *About nine seconds* survives only as a man's own established tempo or Tomas measuring himself. **Tamsin Rook's instrument is the only thing that still runs on a nine-second metronome, because it is hers and she built it that way, and that is characterisation and not a tic. AND MEASURE THE FAMILY AND NOT ONE MEMBER OF IT: across Batch 0003 the family was *about the* 50, *about a* 7, *about nine* 3, *about two* 1, *about four* 1 — sixty-eight instances of the word before anything in the ten chapters. A fall in one member of a family is not a fall in the tic; it is a change of which member you happened to be counting, and *about forty* and *about two* are the same tic wearing different numbers and went unswept for four volumes. Report the family. A pass that quotes *about nine* on its own is quoting half a measurement.**

**Nobody in this batch is wicked. Being right is not a door, and nobody is thanked. THE REVERSAL WAS EARNED AT 0176 AND IT IS NOT RE-EARNED HERE: a true sentence has been proved in four mouths in a day and a district's return will live forty years, and no chapter may go back and prove it again.**

**The prose is the thing.** Natural paragraphs, generally two to six sentences. A one-line paragraph only as a deliberate beat. Do not repeat *held*, *steady*, *plain*, *counted*, *breathed*, *wrote* and *nodded* as mechanical filler.

**A TITLE IS A NAME AND NOT A BEAT SHEET, ABOUT TWENTY WORDS, AND IT MAY NOT STATE THE CHAPTER'S FINDING.** Batch 0003's ten titles ran three to seven words, which overcorrects and reads as clipped for its own sake. **Aim at about eight to fifteen words: a name, a place, a piece of work, a person.** It does not tell the reader what the chapter concludes, and it does not contain the word *and* three times. **The ten titles of Batch 0003 are not changed and are not to be changed: they are cited across four state files and they are the record of that batch. This rule is forward and it starts with yours.**
