## The batch in one paragraph

**A man in a city of about nine thousand bells tries to put a true sentence into a bell, and the Office copies the signal and turns it into a warrant, and the warrant is a copy of his own bell, and it is not a lie.** Tomas Vale has spent a hundred and thirty-six days finding out that the instrument he has been refusing since before he left Kells is the only thing in this kingdom that can carry a true sentence out of him in one evening and put it in front of every door — **and that in this city a sound is an hour, and that is the door he has to get through.** **Nobody in this batch is wicked, and that is the engine and it held for all five batches of Volumes 01 and 02 and for Batches 0001, 0002, 0003 and 0004 of this one, and it holds here — including the officer who serves the warrant, including the clerk who copies the signal, including the man who rings the Office's own bell, and including the man who is served.** And a woman standing in a sill in a road takes the worst of what comes through, and loses more hearing, and nobody asks her for a reason including the man who caused it by existing. **And a sister inside the building has to choose between the man she has published an instruction about and a record she is the only one who can hand over, and she chooses the record, and she gives him the means to go, and she does not meet him.**

## The nine things this batch must pay

He is in a room with a form in it.**

## The eleven lines, and the one quotation of them

**They are eleven lines in her own words and the hand in the court copy is not hers.** The one page in this novel that uses the reserved word is inside them, and **that word may appear in this batch only inside a direct quotation of the eleven lines and nowhere else.** The quotation, if a card needs it, is Chapter 0148's and it is in his own hand on his own sheet and nobody else may write it out. **Do not restate the absence of a sentence about what became of her as a list.
