### Chapter 0131 — The Name Of A Thing On A Board Is Not A Person

- **POV:** Tomas Vale. **Location/time:** Sennet, the middle terrace, a market of about nine hundred, a board on a public wall, a step, the arch, Day 117, from the fourth hour into the evening.
- **Goal:** find out what a district is required to do with a sworn answer once it has it, having found out on Day 116 that it does it.
