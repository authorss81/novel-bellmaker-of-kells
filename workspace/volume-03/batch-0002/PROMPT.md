### Chapter 0111 — The Flat, And Nine Hundred Bells Waiting

- **POV:** Tomas Vale. **Location/time:** Sennet, the flat below the water, the wharves, a net-loft, Day 97, from the fourth hour into the evening.
- **Goal:** find out what a city of about nine thousand bells does at the hour, having stood in a yard and watched two hundred people let one of them ring.
- **Resistance:** the flat is not the Office and nobody on it will talk about the top terrace, and **the bells on the wharves are not the Office's either** — they are a wharf's own, a grain wharf's, a net-loft's, and they are struck by men with their own reasons, and the difference between those and a notice-bell is the difference between a keeper and a schedule.
- **Information:** **a bell that is not sounding and is not broken is waiting, and the count of the city's waiting bells is not a number anybody keeps.** About nine hundred bells, about nine hundred written-down reasons, and **a city where the hour is the thing the day is built out of.** A man in a city of two thousand walks into a market; a man in a city of nine thousand comes out into his own yard.
- **Action or decision:** he works a wharf for about two hours for nothing, because a man with a barrow cannot pay for anything and can carry, and **that is the first time in ninety-six days he has done anything useful with his hands and it earns him nothing and gets him a berth and a man's name.**
- **Emotional change:** he finds out that a city is not a town scaled up, and that the coercion he came to look at is **cheaper and duller than a villain** — it is a man on a wharf who goes home at the sixth hour.
- **Power:** none; no perception.
- **Continuity:** no weekday; no month; no official above a district named; the frame stays in its cradle in the open.
- **Ending:** he has a berth, a man's name, and a fact about the wharf bells that turns out to matter in Chapter 0113.
- **Pull:** the top terrace is four streets and four flights up and there is a store behind a door on it.

- **Goal:** look at the store list, and be able to say what is on it in his own name afterwards.
- **Resistance:** **a list that is open is open to anybody who comes, and he has no standing and is not asked for one and is not refused one, and that is the first time in ninety-seven days that an office has not asked him who he is.** And the list is **a list of fittings and a list of districts and a list of numbers, and it does not contain a single person, and he cannot make it do the thing he brought it up here to do.**
- **Information:** **the answer to the third question is not in the list, and the answer to *how an office gets into a bell* is not in the list either, and the list is exactly what a store's list is.** About nine hundred entries, an instruction number against each, a district, a date, and **a column for what the thing is for that is empty from end to end, and he counts the empties and the count is the batch's figure.**
- **Action or decision:** he looks for about two hours, and he asks one question, and the question is not *what is this for*, and **that is the finding: he does not ask it, and he writes down that he did not ask it, and the reason is that a form is a thing you fill in with a reason and he has been eleven weeks finding out that a reason is the hardest thing in this kingdom to get anybody to put their name to.**
- **Emotional change:** **he has come ninety-seven days to a building and the building is a counter and a book and a man, and the man is right, and the answer is that the whole of what he was afraid of is a column with nothing in it, and he cannot use it, and he is not going to write a paragraph about it.**
- **Power:** none; nothing rung; no perception.
- **Continuity:** **no mechanism, no clapper, no named official, no name transferred, no child found by a document; the list is not a fourteenth document and he leaves with nothing; the register stays thirteen.**
- **Ending:** about nine hundred empty cells, one number, and a hearing in two days.
- **Pull:** tomorrow is the day before, and after that a room at the second hour.

### Chapter 0116 — Two People Who Will Write A Thing Down

- **POV:** Tamsin Rook. **Location/time:** Sennet, the middle terrace, a market of about nine hundred, a court, Day 102, from the fourth hour into the evening.
- **Goal:** find out whether a person can be asked to write down a true thing about themselves in a public room, and what it costs.
- **Resistance:** **the leash is used a ninth time — or is not. The card's instruction is that it is NOT to be used, and the non-use is the chapter.** And the resistance that is on the page is a real one: **a person may be asked and may refuse, and a person who writes a thing down in a public room becomes a person who is read by that room for the rest of their life, and about four hundred people a year in this city already do it for money.**
- **Information:** **two people she has not met will put a true sentence in a document in their own names, unprompted, about their own business, and neither is thanked, and neither is a hero, and neither is owed anything.** One is a market keeper with about nine rows who has a book of his own and no office. **A claim said twice is the only thing in this kingdom that has ever moved a line, and it takes about nine seconds, and it moves about four in ten, and it cost her her cover in Chapter 0106 and she is doing it again on purpose.**
- **Action or decision:** she does not ask either of them to do anything. **She says a claim back, four inches, at ten feet, in the ordinary voice, and then she goes and stands about nine feet off, and two people she has never spoken to do a chore for themselves on their own day, in their own yard and at their own pitch, and she is not in the room when they do it.**
- **Emotional change:** she finds out that **the road has finally found a place after all, and it is a queue, and she knew that on Day 84, and what she did not know is that a queue can be walked into by two strangers at once and that neither of them has to be asked.**
- **Power:** none; **her bell is not rung and may be rung at most once, on her own terms.**
- **Continuity:** **nobody asks her for a reason at any hour, including him**; her four columns stand; the fourth has two lines in it and she has not improved them; **the leash is at eight uses and this is not a ninth.**
- **Ending:** two documents with two names at the head of them, and she is nine feet off, and she is not thanked and did not want to be.
- **Pull:** tomorrow is the hearing and neither of the two has met him.

### Chapter 0117 — The Day Before A Room

- **POV:** Tomas Vale. **Location/time:** Sennet, a step at the foot of the top, a yard, Day 103 **at the second hour** — **the hearing itself is NOT in this chapter and the card is about the hour before and the hour after** — from the fourth hour into the evening.
- **Immediate goal:** find out what a hearing is, from the outside, from a man who has been to one.
- **Resistance:** **a hearing is a room where a man is asked to produce things, and the procedure is correct, and the office is right about the standing and wrong about nothing, and there is no form in this kingdom on which a man with thirteen documents none of them his own can be given a hearing at all.**
- **Information:** **a claimant is summoned to produce, a witness is not** — and that distinction is the Office's invention in Chapter 0108 and it is the reason the whole of this batch has been building toward a room. And: **a man who goes into a hearing with nothing in his hands is a man who will be asked *where is it*, and there is no sentence in this kingdom for that answer, and there is no box for it, and about four thousand people a year have never had to find out.**
- **Action or decision:** he does not go in. **He stands about nine feet off a door for about four minutes and then goes and does the only thing he can do with nothing, which is write down, in his own hand, with the hour, the true sentence he will say, and the three things he does not know, and his own fault — and he strikes out one line of it, and the line he strikes out is the one that would be a household's.**
- **Emotional change:** he finds out that **preparing a hearing is preparing a bell, and the difference is that a bell cannot be edited after it has been rung, and he is about to stand in a room and be edited**, and that this is the first time in the whole of his working life that the thing he makes has been made by somebody else.
- **Power:** none; nothing rung; no perception.
- **Continuity:** **all five clauses stand; no document is produced, given, or asked for; the register stays thirteen; the hearing is set up and not entered, and its outcome belongs to Batch 0005.**
- **Ending:** a sheet of his own hand with four lines on it and a fifth struck out, and the fifth is the only one anybody in that room would have wanted.
- **Pull:** a room at the second hour, a form, a man who is right, and a woman nine feet off holding nothing.

### Chapter 0118 — The Man Who Was In The Room Does The Only Thing There Is

- **POV:** Tomas Vale. **Location/time:** the top terrace, the upper room, the yard, Day 104, from the fourth hour into the evening.
- **Goal:** find out what happened in the upper room yesterday, having not been in it, from the one man who was and will not say it twice.
- **Resistance:** **nobody will say it to him in a way he can write down, because a man who says a true thing in a recorded room and then says it to a stranger is a man who has said it twice, and the second time is the one that is used against him.** And the man who was in the room is right, and is not sorry, and is not a door, and will not say it again.
- **Information:** **a hearing is a room where one person's accepted answer is entered as the answer of a whole matter, and it is done in about four seconds, in the correct form, out of the correct column, by a man doing his job, and it is what a clerk's roll does and it is done in a capital instead of a town.** **The batch's finding: the machinery Volume 02 found in four counties is the same machinery and it has a room, and the room is not the point.**
- **Action or decision:** **the man who was in the room on the hundred and third puts a hand against a line on the hundred and fourth, on his own day, for himself.** Not a copy. A hand. He was in the room yesterday and he has not told anybody what was said in it except to one stranger who is asking him nothing useful, **and he has gone and done the only thing available to him, which is to put his own hand on a line in his own name in a room of his own choosing.** It costs that man about four days of his working life and about a hundred yards of corridor, and nobody persuades him, and Tomas did not ask and was in the arch four streets away and is told about it afterwards and is not going to be told it was worth it.
- **Emotional change:** the thing he has spent ninety-eight days looking for turns out to be **a man doing a chore for himself**, and it is the same act as Vey's yard on the seventy-ninth, and **it is not scalable and it is not a network and it is the only thing that has ever moved a line.**
- **Power:** none.
- **Continuity:** **nobody is a villain; the clerk is right; Tomas is the reason and is not going to be told it was worth it; the register stays thirteen; no child is found by a document.**
- **Ending:** a man who is right, about four days, a hand of his own on a line, and a form in a file that is not about anybody.
- **Pull:** a warrant is not yet a warrant, and there is a public bell in Sennet and nobody has rung it, and a hearing has been had.

### Chapter 0119 — The Thing That Was For A Clerk And Not For The Man

- **POV:** Tomas Vale. **Location/time:** Sennet, the middle terrace, a market, a court, a counter, Day 105, from the fourth hour into the evening.
- **Goal:** find out what a hearing he was in leaves behind, in a city of about nine thousand people who were not in the room.
- **Resistance:** **a thing that happened in a room in a capital does not travel to a market in a capital.** Nobody in the market has heard of it, and about four hundred of them are saying names back to each other in a queue anyway, and a man with a book of his own and no office is telling a stranger what it costs to be named, and **he is telling it about somebody else, because Tomas Vale's name is on a board in a market four days back and that is a thing he can be told about and is not being told about.**
- **Information:** **a public record of a hearing is a public record of the office's own account of itself.** It is filed, it is available, **and about four thousand people a year bring a paper to a counter and ask what it is and not one of them has ever asked for the record of a hearing they were not in.** The finding: **an office publishes nothing, and a court publishes, and the difference is four inches long, and it is the only thing in this volume that anybody could be said to have got wrong in their own favour.**
- **Action or decision:** he does not try to make the market know. **He goes and reads the record of his own hearing, because it is the only document about himself in the kingdom, and it is the office's account of him, and he reads it standing up in about eleven minutes and it takes about four hundred words, and the last entry in the office's own hand is that the person named produced nothing.**
- **Emotional change:** **he has wanted a document of his own for ten days and there is one and it is four hundred words about him written by somebody else, and it says he produced nothing, and it is the only paper in this kingdom that can be read as him, and it is an inventory.**
- **Power:** none.
- **Continuity:** **the register becomes a question, and the answer is that a record of a hearing is a public document and is not evidence he carries and is not a fourteenth, and the chapter must keep the two figures apart in one sentence the way Chapter 0095 kept the receipts apart from the thirteen**; all five clauses stand; no clause was broken by attending.
- **Ending:** four hundred words, and *the person named produced nothing*, and a bell in a street in this city that nobody has rung.
- **Pull:** a public bell in a city of about nine thousand, and a frame in a cradle four streets and four flights down, and Batch 0005 is the batch in which one of them is rung.
