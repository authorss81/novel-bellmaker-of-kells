## Chapter cards

### Chapter 0071 — Down The Water

- **POV:** Tomas Vale.
- **Location/time:** the river, Day 56 into Day 57, morning into the second day.
- **Immediate goal:** Get himself, a barrow, a locked frame in a cradle and a certified bell in a crate to a town nobody has named, without arriving as a man with a story.
- **Resistance:** **The last two days of a river are the two days in which a man decides what he is going to be when he gets there, and Chapter 70 took his appetite away, and this is what is left.** A boatman asks him a question about a crate and he cannot answer it, because **the honest answer is that he bought a bell to say something with and he does not know what, and a man who says that on a river is a man who has already told a stranger he has nothing.** And the ordinary resistance of a river: two days is four hours of work, and he is carrying a Crown object he may not ring, a bell he may ring and nobody has heard ring, and about ten sheets of paper, and **a frame that must be bolted at every place it is set down, and a town with no name in it.**
- **Information revealed:** **A clerk's hall is not a district office and a river knows the difference.** Carriers do not take a barrow into a town where there is a clerk's hall, not because they are forbidden and not because they know anything, **but because a carrier's book says what a thing is and a clerk's hall has a man in it who writes down what a thing is, and two books about one barrow is a thing a carrier does not want.** So the answer to how to get a barrow and a stranger and a Crown frame into a town with a clerk's hall is: **you do not go in through the front, and you go in as somebody's business, and you pay for it, and the paying is the entry.**
- **Action or decision:** He goes in as a **carrier's man with a load**, because it is true that he is a man with a load and it is not a lie, and the bell goes in the crate as a smith's consignment, **and the frame goes in the yard and not through the door, and the bolts are made fast where it is set down.** He pays a man to carry a barrow he could not have carried alone, and he pays a man to say nothing, and **he tells Tamsin Rook both facts in advance and she does not stop him.**
- **Emotional change:** He discovers that **the thing he has been doing for five weeks has a cost in shillings, and that the shillings are the only part of it that is honest**, and that a man who has been refused by four offices for nothing has been, on the water, entirely willing to pay a stranger to look away.
- **Power or resource change:** No power. He gains **arrival, a name for the town, and the fact that a clerk's hall is a building with a man in charge of shelves who does not talk to a district officer and does not talk to anybody else either, which Pell Rill told him in advance and which he now believes.**
- **Continuity fact to preserve:** **The frame never leaves the cradle, the bolts are made fast at every place it is set down, and nothing is rung.** The receipt is not broken and **no clause is invoked as a threat**; the fifth clause is a fact about his standing and it is used that way.
- **Ending type:** Arrival.
- **Next-chapter pull:** **A town with a clerk's hall in it has about nine hundred things in it that are not the clerk's hall, and the clerk's hall is the one building nobody can get into, and he has about nine days.**

### Chapter 0072 — The Column Can Be Certified And The Column Cannot Be Certified

- **POV:** Tomas Vale.
- **Location/time:** the town, Day 58, morning into the afternoon.
- **Immediate goal:** Get into the building, or get a person out of it who has been in it, and find out what a clerk's hall actually keeps.
- **Resistance:** **He has no standing, and Rill told him so in advance in a room in another town, and Rill was right.** The man in charge of the shelves is **pleasant and correct**: there is nothing in this kingdom by which a man who is not a district officer may ask a clerk's hall for anything at all, and here is a form if you want to make an application, and the form is a Crown form, and there is no box on it for a reason.
