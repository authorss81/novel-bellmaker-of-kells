## Two house rules, repeated because every batch has broken one of them

1. **No weekday and no month is named in any chapter.** Days are numbered: *the twenty-third*, *the twenty-eighth*, *this morning*. A recurring day of the week is referred to as **a market day, a round, a bell day, or by its number**. Volume 01 ended clean after fourteen lines carrying weekday names were removed across nine chapters, eight of which had been missed by four separate reviews; **do not reintroduce what the audit removed.**
2. **The hour numerals run 1 to 12 twice a day, and no half-day may be attached to an hour that a chapter does not attach one to.** If the prose says *the fourth hour*, then no state file, summary or day-book may say *the fourth hour of the afternoon*. Write the half-day only when the prose gives it. **The Volume 01 close found four such invented half-days and two wrong hour-numbers, all in the state layer and none in the prose, and the chapters were right every time.**

And the rule underneath both: **a fix is not a fix because a summary says so.** Every number, date, clock time, custody state, physical condition, age and name in a state file must be traceable to a specific chapter and line. If a chapter does not give an age, a count or an hour, the state file records that it is not on the page.

## The Chapter 50 state of every object, as written

- **The fixed stripped Crown survey carrier** — out of Kells with Tomas. In its mounting frame, **retaining bolts made fast for transport**, frame in its **reinforced padded cradle**. **No clapper, and the receipt forbids any person from furnishing one — it cannot be rung.** Child-sized wax handprint in the crown, warped lip, a thin copper jumper across a coupling cut nearly through. Released by **Oren Pike** at a custody review **held** at the watch house at about the third hour of the morning of the thirty-third — the receipt was **signed at about the fifth hour** and the frame was brought out of the store at about half past five — on a receipt in **four clauses plus a fifth Tomas asked for**: (1) released as the same object, bolts for transport only, made fast at every place the frame is set down, not to be rung, no clapper furnished; (2) the frame stays in its reinforced padded cradle for the carriage; (3) produced at the request of the district, the watch, or the market court, **and a request may be made by any person and not only by an officer**; (4) **the maker is not the keeper, has no claim, cannot certify it, and may not say what it means**; (5) **the receipt does not make the holder a keeper of any route, no answer to any signal carried on or by the object may be entered by anybody in any book as the answer of a household of this ward, and the holder has no standing to speak for any household in Kells or in any ward.** **The chapter does not say where a copy of this receipt is.** The copy that is in the watch gate book is the copy of the Day 24 request leaf (`ch50:145`), which is a different document. **Chapter 57 of this batch is about clause 3 being used against him. It is not about the receipt being broken.**
- **The plain carrier** — in the **sealed yard** of the Vale Bellhouse, out of its frame, on the bench, inventoried. **Barred by the Bellwrights' Measure on Day 26** from the public practice and from any public place in this county; its origin cannot be certified and no certificate can issue; **the bar is on the bell and not on the licence.** Its two faults stand: too bright and not reducible, and repeating within eight feet of a wall. He is not carrying it and cannot have it back without a custody event. **It is not on the road and is not in this batch.**

Hand-sized, the crown **re-cut inside by a chisel to about a sixteenth of an inch, with a riffle file mark at four points**, and the wax laid over an older signal still in it. A stolen instrument that cannot find its own edge from inside its reach. **Her four terms of the thirteenth day stand.** She rings it in this batch at most once, on her own terms, and never for him.

- **Do not introduce a new final enemy.** **Pell Rill must not appear in this batch in any form** — not present, not quoted, not named on a document, not in a report, not in a margin. ## What this batch must not end with

**Not a coronation, not a cure, not a trial won, not a Crown office defeated.** It ends with a boat, a decision made by other people, eight refusals in eight hands, and a phrase at a landing that nobody explains. The Crown's finding was withdrawn in Volume 01 and nobody has asked for it; Fourmile is a building with a man in it and the man refuses one of four questions in writing; and the volume's engine is **a column, a stamp and a habit**, which is worse than a villain because it needs nobody to be wicked.
