## Batch goal

**Walk Tomas Vale and Tamsin Rook off the flat above Hallowmere and onto Tallow Reach, and get them to a place where a Hearthline node stands unworked, with four people on the road who did not agree to come, and end the ten days with a man who can describe the node and will not describe it in a sentence, and a woman keeping two lists of the same length.**

**The batch's own promise, which is small and must be kept: Tallow Reach is a place people live and a node is a thing that has been kept going by people who are not keepers.** Not a network built, not a node restored, not a chamber reached, not a mother found. **The first half of this volume is a journey and the second half is terms, and neither of them is allowed to be the midpoint, and the midpoint is in Chapters 231 to 240.**

## Starting condition

Day 187, the morning after the last page of Volume 04. **A flat of beaten earth above the water above a staging, above Hallowmere, at about the ninth hour of the evening on Day 186 and now in the morning, on foot, with a barrow and four bolts made fast.** The fixed stripped Crown survey carrier is in its mounting frame in its reinforced padded cradle on the barrow with nothing in it that can strike, **and it has not been in a building since the thirty-third and no chapter of this batch may put it in one.** The certified plain carrier is in a sack against the cradle, **not rung for a hundred and ten days**, four occasions in its life and no more, **and no chapter of this batch may ring it.** His own plain carrier is in a sealed yard in Kells with the bar on the bell and not on the licence and it does not travel. Eleven lines in his own hand are on a leaf of his own book. A district's own form with nine lines in it and no name on it is in a district's own book nine miles behind him and is not in his coat. **The register is thirteen and no chapter of this batch may make it fourteen.** A warrant stands and there is no form on which a man asks for one to be lifted.

**A schedule folded in three is in the inside pocket of his coat with a rectangle about two hands together in it that is now known to be a bay nothing was run into, in the same place in every copy because it is the same frame, and it is not explained and nobody asked the man who could say.**

**No weekday and no month may be named. Every day-time hour is bare, 1 to 12; the ninth hour of the evening is the only half-day and it is the opening hour of the day-book in every one of the ten chapters. The not-asking counter is at fifty days on Day 186, Day 137 is the first day on this road, and it runs fifty-one on Day 187 to sixty on Day 196, one figure per chapter, and no chapter may let it stand still or re-earn the movement. The frame-in-a-building count runs from the thirty-third, a hundred and fifty-four days on Day 187 to a hundred and sixty-three on Day 196. The strike counter runs from the seventy-seventh, a hundred and ten on Day 187 to a hundred and nineteen on Day 196.**

## Batch midpoint

**Chapter 0205, Day 191.** He has been on the reach four days and has proved that Tallow Reach is on no sheet he has ever been shown, **and that the mid-batch turn is a name, not a place: a settlement at the third mile of the reach is on a sheet under a name that nobody at a door uses, and the name on the sheet is the name of a different place four miles up, and both names are correct and neither is a lie.** The turn is negative and it is the point: **the thing he has been carrying for two volumes has never been a picture of where to walk, and on a reach where the ground has two names for every mile the picture stops being an instrument entirely.** He writes that down in his own book in the third column, ruled apart, and he does not improve it.

## Batch climax

**Chapter 0209, Day 195.** The node. Not opened and not described in a sentence: **a chamber, a set of stones, a thing on a frame that is not a bell and is not struck, and a keeper's position with nobody in it that has been empty long enough that the empty place has become a local arrangement.** The man who has kept the thing going for nine years without a standing is a man who will not take the standing when it is offered in words with no form, **and the reason he gives is a fact about a habit and not about a principle: a thing nobody owns gets mended, and a thing a man owns does not, and the asking goes in a book, and a thing in a book stops.** He is right. **Tomas gets no network, no agreement, no keeper and no bell. He gets a chamber he has stood in and a sentence he has been given and a day he has to pay for, and he writes the sentence down in his own book with a bar in it and carries neither half of it to anybody.**

**The last chapter of the batch, 0210, is not a climax. It is the woman with the two lists, and the chapter's job is to make the reader understand that the lists are the same length and that this is not a scandal and is not an oversight.**

## Required character changes

- **Tomas arrives somewhere he cannot read a map of, and the map stops being the thing he reaches for first.** He does not learn to read it. He learns that on ground with two names a mile, a drawing is worse than useless, because it will let him walk to the right place by the wrong name.
- **He asks a person for a thing and is refused, in words, and does not argue.** The batch's fourth refusal is not about a document and is not a villain. **The asking is the growth and the refusal is the cost and neither is improved.**
- **Tamsin is with him and is not a device and is not spent.** She is in her own presence in at least two of the ten chapters, doing her own work, asked nothing. **Her hearing is worse and is hers to price and nobody prices it for her.**
- **A fifth walker may join for a day or two and a fifth walker is still not a company.** Walking the same ground is not agreeing, and the batch says so on the page.

## Required power changes

**None, and the batch may not manufacture one.** No stage, no sense, no perception attempted or had, nothing bound, nothing counted into iron, **no learning where a sound went and no way of learning it.** He cannot fill in a form, cannot enter an answer for a household, cannot certify a bell, and cannot speak for any ward. **The one thing this batch gives him is a fact about feet: he can get to a place he cannot walk to by asking somebody else for a day, and the day belongs to whoever gives it, and he does not own any of it.** That is a habit and not a power and may not be written as one.

## Required world reveals

- **Tallow Reach exists and is nine or ten settlements on nine or ten miles of water, and it is on no sheet Tomas has ever been shown.**
- **A Hearthline node: a chamber, a set of stones, a thing on a frame that is not a bell and is not struck, and an empty keeper's place that people have been working around for years.**
- **A woman keeps two lists — who has said yes to hold a line and who has not — and the two lists are the same length, and she keeps both and tells him so before he asks.**
- **A place can be on a sheet under a name nobody at a door uses, and the name on the sheet can be the name of a different place four miles up, and both can be correct.**
- **A keeper's standing cannot be given in words with no form, because a person with a standing can be put in a box and a man at a lock cannot.**

- **Action or decision:** **he asks the same question at two benches on purpose, gets two true sentences, writes both on one line with a bar between them, and carries neither to the other man — and this is the second time in the novel and it is now the method and not an accident.** He asks to leave the lists with her, and she will not let him, and he does not take them.
- **Emotional consequence:** **he is sixty days on this road with a woman and has not asked her once what it is for, and she is on the step with her hand on a frame and is asked nothing, and neither of those facts is a scene and neither of them is written as one.**
- **Required continuity facts:** **the register is thirteen and nothing was asked for, produced, given or taken on any of the ten days and every day-book says so**; no name is entered anywhere; not-asking **sixty**; strike a hundred and nineteen; frame a hundred and sixty-three; the hand is four and finished and **no chapter of this batch states a fifth**; the ninth hour of the evening twice, opening the day-book.
- **Ending hook:** **he is on the reach with a barrow he cannot pay for and a chamber he has stood in and three sentences he cannot join, and a name in his own hand that he has not said out loud, and the road to where it goes is about four days and there is no form on which a man asks another man for four days of his life.**
