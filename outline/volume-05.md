# Volume 05 Outline: The Hearthline

**Volume number:** 05
**Chapter range:** 201–250
**Created:** by the Volume 04 volume-close audit, **after** the audit passed (`reviews/volume-04/volume-audit.md`, and `PHASE_SYSTEM.md:179`). It is written **against the prose of Volume 04 and not against any summary of it**, and where `outline/series.md` and the prose disagree, the prose governs and the disagreement is recorded below. **`outline/ending.md` is binding on this volume and has no Volume 05 section; where this outline and `outline/ending.md` disagree, `outline/ending.md` governs, and nothing in this volume may contradict it.**

**Where the series outline's Volume 05 paragraph and the prose of Volume 04 disagree, resolved here in favour of the prose, on the Volume 01, 03 and 04 audit precedent.**

1. **The series outline's starting state says *he has learned to ask for help, but still treats his own route as the most reliable one*, and the prose supports the second half exactly and the first half only in one form.** At the end of Volume 04 he has four people on the road, and **the help he learned to ask for was asked in the only form available to him, which is a question with no form on it, and the two of them said no to him, and he walked past the no anyway.** There is no moment in Volume 04 in which he asks a person for a thing and is given it; what there is, four times, is a person giving a day and him not asking why. **He has learned to accept a gift and has not learned to ask for one, and a later volume may not write a scene in which he asks and is helped until this volume has earned it.**
2. **The series outline says he is *no longer able to work alone without collapsing*, and the prose does not say that either.** What the prose says is: **he could not keep his feet for about four minutes after Day 132 and could not hold a straight edge for a day and a half, and on Day 186 he put an edge on the flat and got nine inches out of nine feet before his hand went, and there is no form on which a man can be told that he may not hold one either.** He is not shown collapsing. He is shown working alone at the edge of what his hands will do. **This volume may bring him to the edge and may not put him over it, and the price of the edge is not a wound and is not a fever and is not a fade.**
3. **The series outline's Volume 05 paragraph is otherwise delivered against.** Tallow Reach is not on the page and is a place this volume enters. The local keepers, the Hearthline node and the capital's districts are not on the page and are this volume's material. **The First Answer chamber is opened in this volume and not before, and that is the MIDPOINT and not the climax, and `outline/ending.md` puts the chamber's contents in Volume 08 — so what Volume 05 finds in it is a woman, and what Volume 08 finds is a staffed room of clerks, copied registers, old route stones and a human central relay, and neither volume may be used to unpick the other.**

**Central pressure:** **Tomas and Tamsin go to Tallow Reach to restore a Hearthline node, and the reason they go is not the node.** He is looking for his mother, and the node is the only thing in Tallow Reach that anybody has ever heard of her in connection with, and the local keepers want a network and want it on terms that may not include the capital. **A network is the only instrument in this kingdom that can carry an answer further than a man can walk, and every keeper who has ever been asked to hold one has found out that holding it is not a duty with a shape and refusing it is not an option with one — and Volume 05's question is what a node costs the people who stand under it, not what a node can do.**

## Starting power level

**Tomas is at the first edge of Stage 1 (Near Toll) exactly as at the end of Volume 03, and this volume may not move him.** He has bound one bounded warning in his life and spent it. He does not identify a copied answer, does not perceive, and **is not going to learn where a sound went.** **In Chapter 0146 a forced relay came through him and he decided nothing and cut nothing and gained nothing he can carry, and no volume has told him and this one may not.**

**What this volume gives him instead of a stage, in this order:** a place where a node is and a person who can say what it is for; a person who is not a keeper and will not become one; **and the discovery that a network with a keeper in it is a different object from a network with a name in it.** The growth is **civic, relational and physical** — a man with a map he cannot read, a warrant standing behind him, a road, and a chamber.

**The one question this volume inherits from Volume 04's close is not a power question and must not become one: whether a line can be refused, and what a person who holds a line is obliged to do about it.** It has **three partial answers that do not agree with one another and no conclusion** — a reason off a clerk's mouth while he ruled a line, a price off a man with a scraper on a lock step, and an account off a man coiling a rope. **He asked the same question at two benches on purpose and carried neither answer to the other man, and that is not a failure of the method, it is the method. ## Major locations

- **Tallow Reach.** A reach of water with settlements on it, nine or ten benches, and **a Hearthline node that has not been worked in a long time and that nobody local can describe in a sentence.** The reach is reached on foot and by water and the journey is the volume's first three batches.
- **The Hearthline node.** A chamber, a set of stones, a bell that is not a bell, and a keeper's position that has been empty for long enough that the empty place has become a local arrangement. **It is not a building with a door that opens, and the volume may not make it one.**

- **The capital's districts.** Visible from outside, reachable, **and not to be re-entered as a rescue**: the top terrace's rooms, the store with a book, the board, the yard with the gate frame. **A chapter may put him on its road without putting him in it.**

## Midpoint

**Reached in Chapters 231 to 240 and not anticipated before it, in the shape Volume 02 established with the roll that does not need anybody's answer and Volume 04 established with the panel that has no keeper in it.**

He goes to Tallow Reach to restore a node and finds that the node is not the thing that is broken. **The thing that is broken is that one person at the top of the arrangement is holding an answer that belongs to a reach, and the reach has been answering for itself for years in a form nobody at a door calls an answer, and the woman at the top of it is his mother and has been for twelve years.** **Lira Vale is alive. She is a living anchor for twelve years, conscious in fragments whenever the central bell is rung, and she is not well and she is not coming back with him.** Dain says she chose to remain. **Tomas cannot yet tell whether that choice was freely made or made under pressure, and the volume's discipline is that he does not find out in this volume and that no narrator sentence settles it either.** The reversal is not that she is a prisoner and not that she is a machine: **it is that the choice is real, and that a real choice made inside an arrangement somebody else built is still a thing she has to live inside, and that the two facts do not cancel and neither one is the answer.** The midpoint is paid at the moment he understands that the node he came to restore is a way of asking her to hold an answer for a reach that has been holding its own for years, and that she has been saying yes.

## Volume climax

**Chapters 242 to 250, on the reach and inside the network, and the choice is the reverse of every choice he has made in four volumes.**

A local route fails while Tomas is close enough to cut her free. **He chooses the trapped settlement first, because the local keepers have accepted the risk and said so in advance and because she tells him not to turn her anchor into another private rescue.** He leaves her behind with a promise to return, **and the promise is the first thing he has ever made to her that is not about himself, and the chapter says so in his own hand and does not make it noble.** The cost is named and is not a wound: a settlement's week of somebody else's labour, a route that stays down until a keeper who is not a keeper goes up it on foot, and a reach that will hold a network on terms that do not include the capital. The Tallow Reach Hearthline is restored. **No Crown office is defeated. Dain is not relieved of anything and is not named as defeated. No bell he made is unbarred. Nobody is rescued and nothing is restored, and she is still there.**

## Concrete resolution

By Chapter 250:

- **The Tallow Reach Hearthline is restored**, and **it is restored on the local keepers' terms and the terms do not include the capital.** The refusal of a central command is a fact about a habit and not a principle, and the sentence is the same one the cantons gave in Volume 04 and it is given by different people in a different trade.
- **A settlement is entered as a place people live on a district's own form, in a district's own book, with no name on it anywhere because there is no box for one, and nobody is thanked, and the entry restores nobody's answer.** The Volume 04 form is on nine places up the water from here and this volume does not repeat it; if a form is filled in here it is a district's and it is nobody's business and it costs somebody four days of their own carrying.
- **Lira Vale is alive, is not freed, and has told him two things:** that the original Hearthline had many answers, and that the central chamber is using her memory to impersonate one. **And that freeing her without a replacement may collapse every dependent route.** He does not know what to do with it and the volume does not decide for him.
- **The bay nothing was run into is seen from the other end and is still not explained, and nobody asked the man who would know.** The three partial answers are still three and they still do not agree with one another and there is still no fourth, and **he asked the same question at two benches on purpose and carried neither answer to the other man, and that is the method and not a failure of it.**
- **A warrant stands. A notice comes off a stake because it is done, and a notice goes onto a board because there is a form for it, and the two are different objects.** A man in a chair four days' road away is not thanked and is not owed a visit. A woman inside a building is not met. A bell in a sealed yard in Kells is not unbarred and does not travel, and the Crown notice about an originating workshop is still nailed to the ward board in Kells.
- **The Crown is not defeated.**

## New question for the next volume

**How can a witness be freed without making the network's people pay for her captivity?** Volume 05's resolution is a restored node on terms that exclude the capital, a woman left behind with a promise, and the knowledge that the central chamber is using one woman's memory to stand in for an answer that used to be many. **What none of those can do is free her without a decision somebody else pays for** — every dependent route is somebody's hour, and there is no form in this kingdom on which a person can be told what their hour is worth. **The next volume's question is whether that cost can be made visible before it is paid, and the answer cannot be a cure.**

**A reach at about the ninth hour of the evening, a barrow he cannot pay for, four bolts made fast, and a node that is working on terms that do not include the capital.** Behind him a settlement that is dry because a man who is not a keeper went up a road on foot, and a rate that has gone up and that nine men will set in a fortnight and will not agree about. **A folded schedule in the inside pocket of a coat with a rectangle in it that four men looked at and one mould maker could not explain and nobody asked him about, and eleven lines in the other pocket copied out for the third time, and a promise to go back that has no form on it and no day on it and no name at the top of it and a road between him and the making of it.** A woman nine feet off his left shoulder whose hearing is worse than it was ten days ago and who may ring a bell in her bag at most once more in her life, on her own terms, and never for him. **And a man who is thirty-two, in somebody else's mouth and in his own hand, who got nine inches out of nine feet with a straight edge on the last day of the last volume and is not stronger tonight and is not weaker and is going back to a place he has already promised to return to.** **A network that answers is a route somebody is holding, and the holding is the whole of it, and nobody is thanked.**


## Planned power and relationship progression

- **No new stage and no new sense at any point in this volume. No perception is attempted, none is had, and no chapter may describe him reaching for one.** He does not learn to identify a copied answer, does not learn where a sound went, **is not told, is not going to be, and Volume 05 may not hand him the ability to read a map for free or tell him where either of his two public ringings went.**
- **The one permitted change is not a power change.** It is a physical and civic capacity he acquires by practice and cost: **he can get a true sentence to a place he cannot walk to in a day, by other people's legs, and he knows exactly what that costs and cannot make it cost less.** That is a fact about feet and habit and is not a stage and may not be written as one.
- **Tamsin's hearing is not restored and is not rescued, and is worse at the end of Volume 05 than at the end of Volume 04. No chapter may restore any of it, ask her for a reason, hold it over her, treat her as a casualty of his decision, or treat her as a person who has been exposed** — and **she is not to be improved, softened or made sentimental about it in order to make a scene work.**
- **The leash is at eight uses. No ninth use of the slate is staged in this volume, and a non-use is not a use.**
- **The growth is civic, relational and physical:** a man with a map he cannot read, a warrant standing behind him, a road, and a chamber he has been inside once.
- **The cost is not a wound, a fever or a fade.** It is a settlement's week, a route that stays down, a promise he cannot keep on the day he makes it, and a hearing that is worse.
- **The register is thirteen and may not become fourteen**, and the frame is never in a building, and no bell of his is ever unbarred, and **no name is entered on any paper by anybody in this volume unless the paper is a district's own book about its own ground and the name has no box for it.**
