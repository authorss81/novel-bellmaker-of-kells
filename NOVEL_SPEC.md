# Novel Specification

Title: The Bellmaker of Kells
Repository slug: novel-bellmaker-of-kells
Genre: low fantasy / mystery
Male lead: A bellmaker whose bells carry accepted truths
Length target: 550 chapters
Relationship policy: One slow-burn romantic relationship between Tomas Vale and Tamsin Rook, developed through trust, disagreement, and chosen partnership; no harem structure.
Power rule: Growth must be earned through practice, cost, failure, relationships, and changed decisions. The lead must not become instantly overpowered unless the approved genre premise requires it.

## Premise
A bellmaker makes bells that carry promises, warnings, and unfinished apologies, and one bell warns of a crime that has not happened.

## Ending
The bell network is reformed so rulers cannot use it to command every life.
